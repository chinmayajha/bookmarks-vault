## 💬 Essence

Pulkit says the most challenging interview for his HFT offer was multithreading - even more important for senior roles and fintechs - and lists the 20 concurrency problems he solved: Print in Order, FooBar variants, Print Zero Even Odd, Building H2O, Traffic Light Intersection, Dining Philosophers (plus lock-ordering variant), Bounded Blocking Queue, Multithreaded Web Crawler, Fizz Buzz Multithreaded, producer-consumer, thread-safe counter, concurrent hashmap, read-write lock, barrier, countdown latch, thread pool design. His thread points to a Java multithreading course for senior engineering interviews, jokes that interviewers expect you to use every core in the data center, and says most are medium and some hard. Replies joke about earning your second core, plan to try them all, and ask about difficulty; 798 likes and 1,465 bookmarks.
