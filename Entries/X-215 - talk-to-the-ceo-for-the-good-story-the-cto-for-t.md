# 'Talk to the CEO for the good story, the CTO for the real one'

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/SWashishth/status/1554107884090183680)** · `Surabhi Washishth✨` · 2022-08-01 · 63 replies · 3.1K likes

## 💬 Essence

OP's one-liner - 'To hear a good story, talk to the CEO. To hear the real story, talk to the CTO' - drew 3,086 likes and 285 reposts; the reply digest shows the joke getting extended: CTOs say everything is broken even when partly working (which is why they are kept away from VCs), better yet pick companies where the CEO used to be a CTO, then CFO, sales and CMO variants, 'good story from the PM, real story from the software engineer', and 'horror story from the investors'.

## 💡 Takeaway

Light workplace humor with strong reply-participation - a good example of an extensible joke format.
