# 50 places to launch your startup: HN, Product Hunt, BetaList, Uneed and more

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/Miracle_NNnn/status/2113140729933422877)** · `Miracle Nnamdi` · 2026-10-22 · 171 replies · 283 reposts · 2.4K likes · 6K bookmarks · 255.5K views

## 💬 Essence

Miracle lists 50 places to launch a startup. The top of the list: Hacker News, Reddit, Product Hunt, GeekNews (news.hada.io), whatlaunched.today, Indie Hackers, BetaList, Uneed, Peerlist and DevHunt. The rest are review sites (G2, Capterra, GetApp, TrustRadius), AI tool directories (Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, Toolify, FutureTools), and launch boards such as SideProjectors, Microlaunch, TinyLaunch and Wellfound. A self-reply sells directory submission for 100+ sites. In the thread he says the list is platforms where founders reported getting users, and results vary by product. Replies add trylaunch.ai and justhunt.co, and the top reply says the real step zero is customers.

## 💡 Takeaway

A broad launch checklist. Pick the few that match your audience instead of submitting everywhere.
