# Why magnesium and ashwagandha work better together

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/LeddyLLC/status/2103474098420777302)** · `Leddy` · 2026-09-25 · 26 replies · 1.3K likes · 354.2K views

## 💬 Essence

OP's #ad thread argues each supplement alone fixes only one half of the cortisol problem: magnesium alone only helps overnight clearance (liver enzymes break cortisol down, better sleep, no 3am waking, less puffiness) while daytime production stays high; ashwagandha alone modulates the HPA axis to lower production (calmer, less reactive) but clearance stays slow; together ashwagandha lowers production by day and magnesium accelerates clearance at night. Timing is 'the entire mechanism' - ashwagandha KSM-66 600mg mid-afternoon with lunch, magnesium glycinate 400mg 45 minutes before bed - followed by a week-by-week payoff timeline (sleep deepens week 1-2, calm week 3-4, body composition week 5-8) and a claim that ashwagandha can cut cortisol up to 28%, with product plugs marked #ad. One reply asks whether taking ashwagandha twice a day makes his own timing backwards.

## 💡 Takeaway

Supplement-stack ad dressed as protocol content - useful as a pattern study; treat claims as marketing.
