## 💬 Essence

OP shares the tidbit that Ilya Sutskever gave John Carmack a reading list of roughly 30 research papers with the promise 'if you really learn all of these, you'll know 90% of what matters today' - hosted as a shared Arc browser folder (card: arc.net 'Ilya 30u30'), which his self-thread idly praises.
