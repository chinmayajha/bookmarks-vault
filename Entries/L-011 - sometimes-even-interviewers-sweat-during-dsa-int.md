# Sometimes even interviewers sweat during DSA interviews

**[Open original ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7444665767553490944)** · `Anmol A.` · 2026-03-31 · ♥516 · 7 comments · 1 repost

## 💬 Essence

Anmol flips the perspective: when a candidate proposes an unconventional solution, the interviewer has to prove on the spot why it won't work without wasting the candidate's time hunting edge cases, and even debriefs grill you on why you picked that question and that rating. Replies agree and add nuance — interviewers stick to questions where they know every possible solution, not just shiny ones, and the real signal from an unconventional approach is how well the candidate reasons about trade-offs and edge cases, with the discussion itself often more valuable than the answer. The post also plugs his discounted 1:1 DSA mocks on topmate; 516 reactions with only 7 comments.

## 💡 Takeaway

Useful reframing for candidates: interviewers fear edge-case ambush too, and reasoning aloud about trade-offs is what gets rated — not the trick answer.
