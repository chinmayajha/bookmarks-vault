# Tokenizing audio: 32kHz waveform straight into codes

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/maharshii/status/1804776013857275976)** · `maharshi` · 2024-06-23 · 9 replies · 421 likes · 28.9K views

## 💬 Essence

OP thinks he has found a good way to tokenize audio and says he needs to look further into preprocessing; the self-thread clarifies he is using SNAC only, converting a waveform at a 32kHz sampling rate into tokens/codes - not text-first, not mel spectrograms (the browser jokes are about Opera GX). Replies point to the Tortoise TTS repo (which also uses SNAC) and the librosa library, ask whether he converts to text first, and one asks for suggestions on tokenizing 2D coordinates with temporal dependency.

## 💡 Takeaway

Small build-in-public on audio tokenization with repo pointers and a follow-on research question in replies.
