# SEO heist: 3.6M traffic stolen from a competitor with AI

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/jakezward/status/1728032634037567509)** · `Jake Ward` · 2023-11-24 · 853 replies · 3.7K likes · 7.9M views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP's case study: they exported a competitor's sitemap, turned the URLs into article titles, and generated 1,800 articles at scale with AI - 18 months later capturing 3.6M total traffic and about 490K monthly, with 489,509 in October alone. The self-thread expands the method: find the competitor (brainstorm, Ahrefs/Semrush, manual Google search), fetch their sitemap at domain/sitemap.xml, and require descriptive URLs (how-to-make-soap beats /t/1043423) so the titles map to real keywords, noting tools now automate 90% of what was manual 18 months ago.

Frequently cited programmatic-SEO playbook with the specific numbers behind it.
