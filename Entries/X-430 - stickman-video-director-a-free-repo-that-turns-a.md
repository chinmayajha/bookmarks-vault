# stickman-video-director: a free repo that turns a script into faceless YouTube videos

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/gremlin0x/status/2118648972135039134)** · `Bankai Sauce` · 2026-11-06 · 153 replies · 44 reposts · 759 likes · 780 bookmarks · 30.9K views

## 💬 Essence

Bankai Sauce offers a repo for faceless YouTube channels that he says removes 90% of production issues for short, long and ultra-long videos. The repo, kaomei/stickman-video-director, turns a script into animated stickman videos and automates visuals, voiceover, captions and sound effects. It is free and open source. In his thread he says any art style works if you give a reference, because the point is to copy the workflow. He says Codex is best because it has unlimited image generation, and Claude can assemble the video if you make the images elsewhere. Most replies ask for the link or say his DMs are closed.

## 💡 Takeaway

The workflow matters more than the stickman style. Faceless channels depend on platform policy for AI content.
