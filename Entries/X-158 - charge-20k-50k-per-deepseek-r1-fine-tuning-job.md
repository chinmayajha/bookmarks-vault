# Charge $20K-$50K per DeepSeek R1 fine-tuning job

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/cjzafir/status/1881708227580318090)** · `CJ Zafir` · 2025-01-21 · 92 replies · 2.8K likes · 348.5K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP argues fine-tuning is a blue-ocean service business: DeepSeek R1 is the first open-source model that is consistently good on local servers, so charge companies $20K-$50K per job. His self-thread adds that posting process, limitations and step-by-step guides on X and YouTube floods you with inbound clients, that you deploy the 400GB model on clients' internal servers rather than cloud (with enough GPUs/VRAM), that a wave of wrapper agents is coming, and even floats fine-tuning on business-outcome data to predict which companies will flop. Replies counter that the hard part is finding companies to sell to, that bad fine-tuners blame the base model (data is the real blocker), that hosting costs are opaque without a strong business case, and that AI-wrapper products may surge again.

## 💡 Takeaway

Popular agency-revenue playbook paired with the honest objections about sales and data.
