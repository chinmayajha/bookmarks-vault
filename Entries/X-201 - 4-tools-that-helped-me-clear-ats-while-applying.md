# 4 tools that helped me clear ATS while applying off campus

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/iamsachin_47/status/1734885864378187791)** · `Sachin Pandey` · 2023-12-13 · 15 replies · 576 likes · 88.1K views

## 💬 Essence

OP credits four tools with improving his resume and clearing ATS screens at LinkedIn, ZeoTap and Ramp to get OA links: Skill Syncer (upload resume plus job description, it suggests keywords and gives a match score where above 70 is good), Resume Worded (gives your ATS score), Jobscan (decent but he barely used it) and MonkeyLearn's word cloud generator (paste the job description, extract important keywords, work them in manually) - he used Skill Syncer and the word cloud most. Replies thank him, ask what the ideal JD match percentage is, one asks what ATS even is, and another links an alternative thread.

## 💡 Takeaway

Practical, ranked ATS-optimization stack for off-campus applicants.
