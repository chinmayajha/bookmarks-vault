## 💬 Essence

OP, who just landed a Goldman Sachs engineering summer internship, asks how to convert it into a PPO — what technical expectations, impact and feedback cycles matter. The standout advice: complete every certification program the bank offers and get a 1:1 discussion with each VP you work with every two weeks, so that by the end of the internship all the VPs know you by name — that, says the commenter, is how you secure the offer. OP adds that there's no fixed roadmap (he did DSA and system design), answers a first-year asking how to prepare similarly, and uses several replies to plug his own learning game apex-play.app. Another intern confirms they got the engineering analyst role too.

The concrete PPO formula from a GS insider — certification programs plus fortnightly VP 1:1s until you're a known name — beyond the usual 'network more' advice.
