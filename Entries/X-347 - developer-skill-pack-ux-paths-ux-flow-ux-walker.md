# Developer skill pack: /ux-paths, /ux-flow, /ux-walker and /ux-auto-improve

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/DennisonBertram/status/2118015665890275697)** · `Dennison` · 2026-11-04 · 9 replies · 2 reposts · 57 likes · 130 bookmarks · 7.4K views

## 💬 Essence

Dennison promotes his developer skill pack for Claude, built around four UX skills. /ux-paths writes exhaustive user-journey stories from the codebase with parallel sub-agents. /ux-flow scores each journey for friction and redundancy and files ranked simplification issues. /ux-walker walks every story in a real browser, checks alignment and spacing with a geometry-audit script, and files GitHub issues. /ux-auto-improve loops one app section per round: a cheap reviewer critiques, Opus or Fable judges and argues against, cheap implementers build the survivors with regression tests, and each fix gets a narrated before and after walkthrough video. His suggested run is /goal with ux-paths, ux-flow, ux-walker and ux-visual, then three rounds of ux-auto-improve in /loop. He says it does more than impeccable, but he does not use impeccable. Replies ask how it compares to impeccable and ask for simple before and after demos. The claims of one-shot SaaS apps have no proof in the thread.

## 💡 Takeaway

A UX audit and auto-fix loop worth testing on one app section first. The strongest claims are boasts without evidence.
