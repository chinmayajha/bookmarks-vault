# The 32 TikTok accounts maths: 4 phones to $438K a year, but the inputs are guesses

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/ChadAppDev/status/2122365801445396794)** · `Chad` · 2026-11-16 · 80 replies · 44 reposts · 713 likes · 1.1K bookmarks · 97.1K views

## 💬 Essence

Chad lays out funnel maths: 4 phones run 32 TikTok accounts, which post 3 times a day, or 96 posts. At 1,000 views each that is 96,000 views a day. At 5 downloads per 1,000 views that is 480 downloads. At a 5% pay rate that is 24 paying users, and at $50 a year that is $1,200 a day or $438,000 a year before renewals. In his thread he says he is testing posting straight to live, starting with drafts, and uses the Ampli scheduler. Replies warn that one shadowban can hit every account on the same phone. Others ask how he passes phone verification for 8 accounts per phone, and one reader got 0 downloads from 3,000 views. See X-391.

## 💡 Takeaway

Every rate in the chain is an assumption, not a result. Platform rules on multiple accounts apply.
