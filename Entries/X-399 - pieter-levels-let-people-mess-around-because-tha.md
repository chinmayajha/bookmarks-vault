# Pieter Levels: let people mess around, because that play turned into millions

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/levelsio/status/2122724654989332634)** · `@levelsio` · 2026-11-17 · 44 replies · 61 reposts · 1.3K likes · 479 bookmarks · 114K views

## 💬 Essence

Pieter Levels tells how a pointless game on his couch in February 2025 led to big results. He tried a Mario Kart clone with Claude, then built a flight simulator. Claude 3.7 Sonnet made it work, thousands of people flew it in multiplayer, sponsors paid for in-game blimps, and it made $85,000 in a month and $200,000 in total. A Cursor founder wrote to him by DM for feedback, and he invested in Cursor. He then ran Vibe Jam, a vibe-coded game contest with 1,000+ makers and over 1 million players, and says Cursor was later acquired by SpaceX. His point is to let people mess around and see where it goes. A quoted post tells readers to explain this to partners. A reply says the Vibe Jam site is online at vibejam.com/2025.

## 💡 Takeaway

A founder story with claims we cannot check. The useful idea is that unplanned play can be the research.
