OP releases his 12+ hour course 'Practical Introduction to Natural Language Processing' free on YouTube as the fundamentals path into the LLM, RAG and AI-agent era, covering dataset creation, text-to-vector methods (TF-IDF, Word2Vec, sentence transformers), visualisation, LLM APIs like OpenAI, deploying NLP APIs and LLM SaaS basics.
