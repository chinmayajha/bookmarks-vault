# Why can't I save one job application and reuse it everywhere?

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/airwarmedd/status/2034208913508512029)** · `Aditi Sharma` · 2026-03-18 · 25 replies · 6 reposts · 221 likes · 23.2K views · 54 bookmarks

## 💬 Essence

Aditi vents after 5 Workday applications: why doesn't a tool exist to save your full application once and reuse it across companies when every form asks the same questions? Her thread says she'll try the suggestions and notes she also fills Google/Typeform and records intro videos. Replies offer tools - Simplify chrome extension, Teal, Lazy Apply, greenhouse as an alternative - plus two builders saying they're shipping exactly this (one live by Sunday, tsenta auto-fills Workday forms), and a suggestion to build an agent you verify at the end; 221 likes on 23K views.

## 💡 Takeaway

A validated pain point (repetitive ATS forms) with a crowded tool landscape - a live market test for application-autofill products.
