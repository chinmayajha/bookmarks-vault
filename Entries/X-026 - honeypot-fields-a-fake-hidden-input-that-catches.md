# Honeypot fields: a fake hidden input that catches bots

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/CodeWithTamara/status/2100940633896604051)** · `Tamara Martinović` · 2026-09-18 · 73 replies · 44 reposts · 1.2K likes · 82.9K views · 356 bookmarks

## 💬 Essence

Tamara discovers honeypot fields on website forms: add a fake input, hide it from humans, let bots enthusiastically fill it in - a value means reject, empty means a real person. Her thread chats with repliers about hiding it properly and it being the first line of defense she didn't know existed. Replies add real-world nuance: chrome autofill once filled their 'company' trap field and bounced real people for a week (use a name autofill won't recognise and hide it offscreen), CAPTCHAs also measure solve time and mouse movement, hidden fields can't stop JS-aware bots, and bot traffic now outnumbers human on some sites; 1,188 likes and 356 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

A beginner-friendly web-security classic with practical autofill gotchas - a quick teach-in for form design.
