# OpenDesign Motion makes agent videos with any model, no Opus required

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/OpenDesignHQ/status/2111023212129829086)** · `OpenDesign` · 2026-10-16 · 70 replies · 169 reposts · 1.7K likes · 2K bookmarks · 253.9K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

OpenDesign Motion is an open-source alternative to Claude Motion that decouples motion-video generation from one model: any agent and any model can drive it, and the launch video itself was made in OpenDesign. The thread is a demo reel for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, showing tutorial-style motion graphics, a self-made launch video and multiple demos made without Opus 5.5. The author says it renders locally and new users get free trial credits. Replies split between people wanting to try it and motion designers unimpressed by the output - 'fast cuts with colourful distractions' that ignore motion-graphics principles - which is the same taste gap that shows up in every code-generated video launch.

## 💡 Takeaway

The interesting claim is model independence and local rendering, not the demo quality; treat it as an option to test against Claude Motion rather than a drop-in upgrade.
