# 'Judge me' idea: GPT-4 analyses your tweets to judge your character

## 💬 Essence

No replies and no thread of its own: this tweet is an idea pitched into @krishnerkar's challenge - 'i'm bored, drop some random crazy ideas/experiments to build with GPT-4. i'll pick the one with most likes and ship it in 24 hours'. The proposal is concrete enough to ship in a day: the user enters a Twitter user id, the app fetches that account's posted tweets and liked tweets (images included, since GPT-4 had just gained image support), then judges the person's character from them - 'Name it judge me!'.
