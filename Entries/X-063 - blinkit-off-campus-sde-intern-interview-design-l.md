## 💬 Essence

Anushka shares a friend's off-campus Blinkit SDE intern experience: resume shortlisting, then a technical round built on project discussion and DSA that was highly design and reasoning oriented - why HTTP is insufficient for real-time apps and how WebSockets enable persistent full-duplex push, why MongoDB fits real-time systems (flexible schemas, high write throughput, horizontal scaling), trees/BSTs for leaderboards and order books, and designing a high-frequency order matching system with buy/sell books. Her follow-up tells the 153 bookmarkers to actually read it - they ask about whatever is on your resume, so know it plus fundamentals; replies are mostly 'I don't know this much' and where he applied.
