# Bryel offers a domain-specific model trained from one prompt in seven days

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/KJHMiao/status/2110407267539722624)** · `Kevin Miao` · 2026-10-14 · 18 replies · 15 reposts · 216 likes · 308 bookmarks · 18.7K views · 🎬 · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Kevin Miao pitches Bryel Foundry as model training reduced to one prompt: give it a teacher model and a domain, and within seven days you get a custom model up to 2x cheaper in that domain. The proof point is Coinbase's 9B model, which reportedly beat frontier models in its domain while being 55% faster and costing under $100 to train. Bryel says it has used the same tooling to train models for unicorns and is now opening it to outside teams, with a demo booking link. Replies are interested but probe the obvious weak spot - whether a model trained this way holds up on messy, unseen data - and Miao answers that the harder problem is keeping up as real-world inputs evolve, which is why continuous retraining matters.

## 💡 Takeaway

Model training as a service is getting cheap enough to try; the claim to verify is out-of-distribution robustness, not the headline training cost.
