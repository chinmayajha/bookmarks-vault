# 4 eggs, fruit and whey: breakfast if you earn 1 Lakh+/month

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/AnshulGains/status/1985542332398408044)** · `Dr. Anshul Sadhale` · 2025-11-04 · 72 replies · 83 reposts · 1.4K likes · 65.2K views · 322 bookmarks

## 💬 Essence

Dr Anshul Sadhale prescribes the morning: break 4 eggs in a pan, add a fruit plate and a scoop of whey - if you're earning more than 1 lakh per month this breakfast is not at all expensive, make the family breakfast table look like this and invest in your health. His thread justifies it: 4 eggs is about 24g protein and adequate, aim for 1.5g per kg (~100g) if you train, one ICU bed costs 70,000/night (alcohol, smoking, obesity are common admissions), and whole foods mean you don't need whey (though it's convenient and low-calorie). Replies push back on cost (guava at 120/kg plus 16 eggs works out to roughly Rs 7,000/month), say 4 eggs is a bit much for women, question 750mg of daily cholesterol, and offer a roti-and-milk alternative; 1,397 likes and 322 bookmarks.
