# The ~100-page paper colleges should teach in computer architecture

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/drummatick/status/1811047366331646123)** · `Saurabh Kumar` · 2024-07-10 · 24 replies · 4.3K likes · 444.3K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP wishes colleges taught this roughly 100-page paper in computer architecture, praising how much it covers about cache, OS, threads, the memory controller and how the CPU juggles between them, and recommends it to 2nd- and 3rd-year students as a one-or-two-day read; he links the PDF and admits he wouldn't have taken the course without his degree. Replies debate whether any of it is outdated, point to Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective as what colleges do teach, note UCSD covers it while many Cal State programs don't, argue software engineers should know the hardware, question '113 pages in a day or two', and one says it helped him clear an ARM interview.
