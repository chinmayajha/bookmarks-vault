# Sleep, exercise, no alcohol, no blue light: the distilled protocol

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/aniemmadi/status/1873433921888846242)** · `Ani Emmadi` · 2024-12-29 · 61 replies · 3.1K likes · 672.6K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Replying to Aaron Francis asking whether anyone had distilled all the Huberman/Bryan Johnson/Peter Attia advice, OP lists sleep 8 hours, exercise at least 30 minutes a day, no alcohol (even a single sip), unprocessed high-protein food, no eating 3 hours before bed, avoid blue light - 'profit'. His self-thread adds that physical health spreads positivity and that many report feeling happier emotionally after starting to work out, and clarifies the warning is about ultra-processed and canned foods. Replies add morning sunlight, Bryan Johnson's olive-oil supplement tip, 'fix emotional health first because nothing else matters', missing supplements, and skepticism about the blue-light claim.

## 💡 Takeaway

The most endorsed distillation of the longevity-podcast stack, with practical additions in replies.
