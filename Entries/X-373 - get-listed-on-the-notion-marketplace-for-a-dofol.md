# Get listed on the Notion Marketplace for a dofollow link (DR 90)

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/tarasshyn/status/2114368719488967038)** · `Taras` · 2026-10-24 · 12 replies · 5 reposts · 87 likes · 182 bookmarks · 14.8K views

## 💬 Essence

Taras explains how to list a SaaS in the Notion Marketplace for a dedicated page and a dofollow link. Step 1: build a simple use case that sends data from your SaaS into Notion, such as new leads or reports as rows in a database. Step 2: create a public connection in Notion's developer settings with the installation scope 'Any workspace', which cannot be changed later. Step 3: on the connection page, click Publish to Marketplace and fill in the name, description, category, logo and screenshots. Step 4: submit; Notion replies by email in 5 to 10 business days. The page goes live at notion.com/connections/your-name. Customers can also connect your SaaS to Notion in a few clicks. Replies: one founder says Notion was one of the hardest integrations but worth it, another warns that 'Any workspace' is a one-way choice. Taras says one listing mostly gives an SEO bump, and combined listings bring signups. See the SaaS directory playbook entry.

## 💡 Takeaway

A concrete listing recipe. Check the scope setting before you publish, because it cannot be changed.
