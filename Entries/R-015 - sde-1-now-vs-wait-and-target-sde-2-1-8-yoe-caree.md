# SDE-1 now vs wait and target SDE-2 (1.8 YOE) — career confusion

**[Open original ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/LeetcodeDesi/comments/1r9kusw/sde1_now_vs_wait_and_target_sde2_18_yoe_career/)** · `u/Master_Woodpecker447` · 2026-02-20 · ↑23 · 8 comments

## 💬 Essence

OP, at 1.8 YOE in a repetitive role with little ownership, asks whether to switch now into an SDE-1 role at a stronger engineering org to rebuild fundamentals, or stay and study for an SDE-2 shot directly. Replies lean toward acting now: one says the bar keeps rising and companies expect HLD, LLD and solid dev skills even from 1-2 YOE candidates, so start applying and preparing immediately; another advises staying put for an internal promo first if his current comp and company tier are good, then moving as SDE-2. Reality-check comments note people getting zero callbacks after 3-4 months of applying, and OP discloses he's at a tier-1 bank with a 15 LPA CTC.

## 💡 Takeaway

A crisp articulation of the SDE-1-vs-SDE-2 dilemma with the key counterpoint: the interview bar for juniors now includes HLD/LLD, so waiting has a cost.
