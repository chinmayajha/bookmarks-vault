# Sell 'lifetime' clones of overpriced SaaS on AppSumo

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/CEOLandshark/status/1951018530536825309)** · `Pyrate` · 2025-07-31 · 12 replies · 1.4K likes · 206.6K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP's advice for broke technical people: clone an overpriced product like ManyChat and sell it lifetime - ideally $297, not $1000 - give affiliates 30%, and launch on AppSumo-type sites rather than building another forgettable indie-hacker app; he adds a second play, outsourcing menial tasks you do at a $3/hour valuation to Indian devshop owners who are worse at them. Replies say lifetime deals have been some of their best software investments, but push back that the problem is never the product, it's marketing, that classic lifetime deals shut down months later, and that server and update costs are exactly why subscriptions exist.

## 💡 Takeaway

Contrarian AppSumo lifetime-deal playbook plus the strongest objections to it, in one thread.
