# Quant Trader vs Quant Researcher Is a Bad Map. Here's a Better Taxonomy

**[Open original ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/quantfinance/comments/1uc7aug/quant_trader_vs_quant_researcher_is_a_bad_map/)** · `u/Zealousideal-Fig9666` · 2026-06-22 · ↑213 · 13 comments · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP argues that sorting quants into QT/QR/QD buckets is meaningless because the same title is a different job at every seat — a Jane Street trader codes all day, a Tower trader looks like a full-stack researcher, a junior DRW trader is basically a dev, a quantamental QR running a book is a trader. Instead he proposes five real axes: asset class, holding frequency, where alpha comes from, which pipeline stages you own, and firm/pod structure (with pipeline ownership and org structure the hardest to escape later). Replies broadly endorse the framework as better than the standard buckets — 'titles don't matter, what drives PnL does' — while asking where Jane Street, prediction markets and macro seats fit, and one contrarian says the original QT/QR/QD frame was simpler. The post links out to quantvault.org firm profiles as supporting reference.

## 💡 Takeaway

The most useful 'map of quant roles' framing for anyone choosing a seat: evaluate by alpha source, frequency and pipeline ownership instead of job title.
