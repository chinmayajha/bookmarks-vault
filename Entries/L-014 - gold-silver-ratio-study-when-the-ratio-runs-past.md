# Gold-silver ratio study: when the ratio runs past +1σ, silver outperforms

**[Open original ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7438809888224546816)** · `Sid Chandra` · 2026-03-15 · ♥11

## 💬 Essence

Sid shares a small quantitative study of the gold-silver ratio using daily futures data: long-run mean near 73 with a standard deviation around 14, current ratio around the 20th percentile of history, and the key findings that extreme deviations persist far longer than simple mean-reversion assumes, while ratios above +1σ have historically preceded stronger silver returns over the following 30-90 trading days. His conclusion is that the ratio does revert but the adjustment is slow and unpredictable — 'equilibrium is real, patience is the price of convergence' — and he links the code and analysis.
