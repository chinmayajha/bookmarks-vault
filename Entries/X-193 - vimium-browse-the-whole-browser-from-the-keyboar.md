# Vimium: browse the whole browser from the keyboard

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/knlmsh/status/1773444960890593309)** · `Kunal Mishra` · 2024-03-28 · 2 replies · 37 likes · 32.8K views

## 💬 Essence

OP shares a productivity discovery from a senior in his college's CS lab: install the Vimium Chrome extension and press f on any page to label every clickable element, then hit the label's keys to click it - 'why did I not know this earlier?'. Replies ask whether it just keeps you on the keyboard and suggest trying QuteBrowser instead.

## 💡 Takeaway

Small but genuinely life-changing browser extension; two replies point to alternatives.
