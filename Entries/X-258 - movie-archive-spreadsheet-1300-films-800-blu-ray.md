**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/vilibazmiooo/status/2104323759339655446)** · `vilibazmio` · 2026-09-27 · 166 replies · 10.1K reposts · 114.1K likes · 2.8M views · 155.9K …

## 💬 Essence

vilibazmio's updated public spreadsheet (card: docs.google.com / vilibazmio_archive.xlsx) claims 1,300+ archived movies, nearly 800 Blu-ray remuxes and links that 'will never expire'. The self-thread pre-empts two criticisms: uploads have been live over a year and 'films that aren't mainstream almost never get copyrighted', plus a clarification that the archive 'isn't meant to be used as a streaming platform' - download before watching, and the preview video it sometimes generates is lower quality. Replies split between gratitude and legal unease: snvy asks 'what is the copyright status of these movies?' and warns that enough copyright-holder demands could force archive.org to lock the uploader's account, while Sarvar reports a broken entry ('The Pear Tree (1998) by Dariush Mehrjui... is just an image of the film'). Engagement dwarfs the debate: 114,149 likes, 155,945 bookmarks, 10,079 reposts, 2.8M views, 166 replies.

## 💡 Takeaway

One evergreen spreadsheet outperformed almost everything in this set - durable, save-worthy link assets work; note the obvious copyright risk before emulating it.
