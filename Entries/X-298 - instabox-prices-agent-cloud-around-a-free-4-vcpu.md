# InstaBox prices agent cloud around a free 4 vCPU machine and cheap unit economics

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/hanghuang_/status/2112299795549143378)** · `Hang Huang` · 2026-10-19 · 123 replies · 7 reposts · 132 likes · 78 bookmarks · 6K views

> ⏰ **Important:** InstaBox early users keep five free machines until 1 Dec 2026. Check your machines before then. · ⏳ by 2026-12-01

## 💬 Essence

Day 1 of InstaBox's 28-day 'test in prod' series is the pricing page: one free 4 vCPU / 8 GB machine with no usage limit, plus what Hang Huang calls the most competitive unit price on the market. He credits oversubscription and high utilization for the low cost, and explicitly copies Cloudflare's two principles - free users do QA and force a more intuitive product. 1,000 more invite codes go out; early users keep five free machines until Dec 1, and a Grok bot picks commenters to DM. The 123 replies are almost entirely invite-code begging and use-case pitches (Godot games, agent hosting), with little pricing scrutiny.

## 💡 Takeaway

A candid look at pricing dev infra around a generous free tier and utilization economics - useful as a pricing-strategy read, not as independent proof of the 'best unit price' claim.
