## 💬 Essence

OP, laid off from an unknown company, landed Google and Atlassian offers after months of rejections and distills what mattered in onsites: communication, problem-solving approach and correct, clean implementation — plus a core rule that you only truly understand a solution after writing its code under interview conditions, never from reading. For Atlassian he got exact previously-practiced questions (including a middleware router) from a LeetCode high-frequency discussion post, and he recommends recent high-frequency lists as the highest-leverage resource when time-constrained, re-solving hard ones blind when time allows. Replies include a discouraged job-seeker whom OP encourages (599 upvotes overall), questions about his LeetCode volume and Google's system-design rounds, confirmation he's SF-based, and OP offering to answer DMs; he says LeetCode and similar sites for recently-asked questions were the most useful.

## 💡 Takeaway

High-signal offer-post lessons: always code out solutions, mine recent high-frequency question lists, and expect repeat questions at firms with small question banks.
