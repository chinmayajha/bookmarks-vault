# Six flat-fine legal traps for vibe-coded apps: COPPA, Google Fonts, CAN-SPAM and more

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2117745658040136129)** · `Aakash Gupta` · 2026-11-03 · 21 replies · 29 reposts · 284 likes · 756 bookmarks · 31.1K views · 🎬 · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Aakash Gupta warns that a vibe-coded app can face a six-figure legal bill before the first sale. His list of flat per-violation risks: COPPA, Google Fonts, session replay, CAN-SPAM, auto-renewal rules and the 6 dollar DMCA copyright agent registration. The video is built to be sent to Claude with the request to fix everything. A self-reply lists the sources: FTC civil penalty notice and CAN-SPAM guide, 16 CFR 1.98, LG Muenchen I 3 O 17493/20, Cal. Penal Code 637.2, 17 USC 504(c) and 512(c), and 37 CFR 201.3. It says this is not legal advice. Replies add useful corrections: COPPA applies only if the app targets children or knows a user is under 13, CAN-SPAM has no private lawsuits, and the Google Fonts case is a German ruling. Another reply confirms it is real if you have German visitors, and the fix is to self-host fonts. Two replies want a text file because agents cannot watch video.

## 💡 Takeaway

A good pre-launch checklist, but the worst-case numbers are not typical. Self-host fonts, check COPPA scope, and read the replies' corrections.
