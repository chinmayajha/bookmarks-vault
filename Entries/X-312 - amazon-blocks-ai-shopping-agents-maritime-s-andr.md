# Amazon blocks AI shopping agents; Maritime's Android MCP stays logged in for $1/month

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/maritime_sh/status/2110756701615817045)** · `Maritime (YC F26)` · 2026-10-15 · 2 replies · 2 reposts · 18 likes · 10 bookmarks · 1.3K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

Amazon blocked AI assistants such as Muse, Dots and Instinct from browsing and buying on its site. Maritime pitches a workaround: its Android MCP spins up a persistent Android phone in the cloud, shops on Amazon for the customer, and stays logged in for next time - for $1/month per agent. Replies are sparse; Hunch says it can still use Amazon through a direct connector. The post is a small but clear signal that commerce platforms are starting to fight agent traffic, and infrastructure layers are routing around it.

## 💡 Takeaway

A useful data point on the coming agent-vs-commerce-platform access fight - and a reminder that 'persistent logged-in phone' workarounds carry account and ToS risk.
