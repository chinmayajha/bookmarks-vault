# Reproduce an arXiv paper badly, open source it - beats any certificate

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/asmah2107/status/1980267508641984633)** · `Ashutosh Maheshwari` · 2025-10-17 · 40 replies · 152 reposts · 2.9K likes · 220K views · 1.6K bookmarks · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Ashutosh's loop: find a paper on arXiv, build a shitty version of it, open source it on GitHub, write a post explaining why it's shitty - 'that GitHub repo is 100x more valuable than any certificate' - quoting his own take that no AI engineer in their 20s became a 10x developer by just collecting Coursera certificates hoping tutorial magic lands them a job at OpenAI. His thread says just share your work publicly and invites people to post theirs. Replies ask how to frame it for students (detail limitations plus an improvement roadmap), warn the exercise loses its benefit if you do it 100% with Claude Code or Codex, ask whether courses and projects must be a dichotomy, and call it the most honest roadmap they've seen; 2,872 likes and 1,623 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

Portfolio-over-certificates advice for aspiring AI engineers, with the caveat that agent-coding the exercise defeats the purpose.
