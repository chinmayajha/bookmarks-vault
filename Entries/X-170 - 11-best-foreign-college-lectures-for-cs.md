# 11 best foreign college lectures for CS

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/cneuralnetwork/status/1868649779263426672)** · `neural nets.` · 2024-12-16 · 40 replies · 3.8K likes · 296.5K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP lists 11 of the best foreign college lectures for CS: Oppenheim on signals, MIT 6.006 intro to algorithms, Jack Buffington on digital logic, MIT 6.042J maths for CS, CMU computer architecture (Spring 2015), MIT 18.404 theory of computation, Berkeley CS162 OS, CMU 15-445 databases, a CSU Sacramento compilers course, CMU CS340 computer networks, and Stanford CS229 machine learning. Replies include Perplexity's Aravind Srinivas saying these are 'more essential than ever before', praise for the legendary CMU database course, a claim the 2010 MIT discrete-math version is better, an IIT Delhi alternative for OS and compilers, and jokes that the list will be copied to LinkedIn.
