# Spotify launches Xirp, a vendor-neutral agentic dev environment

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/SpotifyEng/status/2086795659651191106)** · `Spotify Engineering` · 2026-08-10 · 636 replies · 2,063 reposts · 11.6K likes · 5.3M views · 9,457 … · 🎬 · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Spotify Engineering launches Xirp, a vendor-neutral agentic development environment that manages agent sessions across Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Codex in one place, already used by 1,300+ Spotify engineers and now available for anyone to try; the self-thread links Tyson Singer's piece ('Spotify Portal - One source of truth for engineers & agents'). The captured replies are mostly incredulity rather than adoption: 'Spotify? The AI Company?', 'would it sing to me while i code?', 'Spotify building an IDE wasn't in my bingo card', 'Why is Spotify trying to hop on the AI bandwagon', kitze's 'Bro stay in your lane and fix your... music app', and 'what the fuck lol'. A few are substantive - Jason Kneen asks 'Closed source? Seriously?' (nothing in the launch claims open source), Marcelo Lima wants a way to code his own Spotify UI, Teknium wants to see Hermes on it, Logan Juhl jokes about everyone's 'Xirp Wrapped'. Still the biggest reach in this set: 636 replies, 2,063 reposts, 11,566 likes, 9,457 bookmarks, 5.3M views.

## 💡 Takeaway

Vendor-neutral session management across Claude, Gemini and Codex is the layer large orgs are standardizing on - Spotify open-sourcing it is a strong signal for multi-agent tooling.
