# Phonon-2: 164MB speech-recognition model beating Whisper large

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/yoitsmanan/status/2104990913886031993)** · `Manan Gupta` · 2026-09-29 · 219 replies · 511 reposts · 6.1K likes · 538.5K views · 7.3K bookmarks · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Manan Gupta announces Phonon-2 from fermion_ai: a 164MB open-weights (CC-BY-4.0) speech recognition model that is on average more accurate than OpenAI's Whisper large, a model 10x its size, and transcribes an hour of audio in 20 seconds on a MacBook Air. The thread adds it keeps 99.8% of its 2.5GB teacher's average word accuracy in just 6.5% of the bytes, runs at 174x real time on a base M5 Air (6,680x on H100 with batching), and powers the free Detta dictation app with Gluon text polishing at 250ms median latency, fully local. A serious edge-speech-model release: tiny model, near-teacher accuracy, free local dictation app - worth tracking for on-device voice features.
