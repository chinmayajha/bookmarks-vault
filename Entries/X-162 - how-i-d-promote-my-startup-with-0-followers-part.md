# How I'd promote my startup with 0 followers (Part 3)

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/natiakourdadze/status/1879565960098832641)** · `Natia Kurdadze` · 2025-01-15 · 28 replies · 702 likes · 130.8K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP's seven-step zero-audience promotion plan: auto-submit to 100 business directories via boringlaunch.com for initial traffic; win clients with Lemlist's LinkedIn voice outreach (filter ICPs by title/location, automate connection requests); mine the Facebook Ads Library for people who commented on competitor ads and DM them; find micro-influencers with Eezycollab's free trial by engagement rate; drop thoughtful advice plus your link in Hacker News comments (unlike posts, comments allow links); warm up Reddit leads from big niche communities with a non-spammy opener; and a pitch for her paid startup accelerator. One reply recommends getseer.com for the same social-listening angle since X and Reddit don't make search easy.
