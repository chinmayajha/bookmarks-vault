# DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is free in OpenCode; Kuvo Code runs bot teams on it

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/beentherebtw/status/2111738514043724258)** · `serhat` · 2026-10-18 · 11 replies · 11 reposts · 297 likes · 262 bookmarks · 19.4K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

Serhat points out that DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is free on OpenCode right now, so you can run a whole team of bots in Kuvo Code at zero cost. Kuvo's bots now hold standups: a lead splits the work and each bot executes its part. He clarifies that Kuvo uses the OpenCode engine, works offline, and does not require an OpenCode account; the recent OpenCode breach affected only Console users. Replies are wary - the breach, aggressive rate limits, model-switch crashes, and questions about Windows/mobile support dominate.

## 💡 Takeaway

A free way to experiment with multi-agent 'standup' workflows, with the honest caveat that free-model rate limits and OpenCode's breach are real risks.
