# Run LinkedIn ads on every potential investor for a month before raising

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/GaddipatiHarsha/status/2109130292602802577)** · `Harsha from Slashy` · 2026-10-11 · 23 replies · 21 reposts · 1.1K likes · 1.3K bookmarks · 264K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Harsha's underrated fundraising alpha: before raising, run LinkedIn ads against every single potential investor for one month (about 5k spend) to manufacture familiarity and FOMO. His follow-up adds they got VC inbound accidentally the same way through paid email clients, noting a good chunk of paid-email users are VCs. Replies split: a top quote-tweet calls it the top of dumb ideas, one says 'Delete this' (keep the alpha secret), others ask about conversion proof, whether it is illegal (tagged to Grok), and joke it only works if investors are in high school; 23 replies, 1,085 likes, 1,255 bookmarks, 264K views.

## 💡 Takeaway

A cheap pre-raise familiarity play - worth testing with a tight investor list and reply-rate tracking rather than assuming FOMO.
