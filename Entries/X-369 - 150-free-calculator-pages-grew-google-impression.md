# 150+ free calculator pages grew Google impressions from 780 to 66,000 a day

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/junead_kh/status/2115518966852399219)** · `Junead Khan` · 2026-10-28 · 41 replies · 18 reposts · 279 likes · 478 bookmarks · 33.7K views

## 💬 Essence

Junead built 150+ free calculator pages: a paycheck calculator, an income tax calculator and tax brackets for every US state (treasury.sh/tools). Daily Google impressions went from 780 to 66,000 in 6 weeks. The Search Console screenshot shows 5.28K clicks and 1.66M impressions over six months, 0.3% CTR and average position 7.5, with the jump starting in late September. In replies he checks indexing: 720 pages indexed and 58 'discovered, not indexed', 56 of them calculator pages. Conversions are small, because people searching 'paycheck calculator' want an answer, not an app. Adding a call to action at the bottom of each calculator started to bring signups. Replies advise watching indexed versus submitted pages in Search Console, because Google may drop templated pages, and ask about revenue.

## 💡 Takeaway

Programmatic SEO with real utility pages works for traffic. Monetizing the visitors is the open problem.
