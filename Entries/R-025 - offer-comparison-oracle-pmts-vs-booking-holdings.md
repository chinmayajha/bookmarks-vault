## 💬 Essence

OP with 6.5 YOE (currently banking, 54 LPA total) lays out three offers: Oracle Health PMTS/IC4 in Bangalore at ~84 LPA first year (51.8 base + $75k RSU), Booking Holdings SDE-2 at ~81 LPA (59 base, 15% bonus, $20k RSU + $20k sign-on equity), and Arista Pune at ~70.5 LPA. The 111 replies decisively favor Booking: the top comment (102 upvotes) warns Oracle has made dumb investments it needs to fund and Oracle Health is where they'd trim fat, plus Booking's levelling is odd but SDE-2 sits close to a good band; Oracle insiders say skip it and that the culture isn't great, while Booking people confirm the process (referral → HackerRank OA on implementing APIs in Spring Boot → 3 rounds). A commenter notes he earns the same at JPMC London with 7 YOE as an SDE-3 on 61k GBP, and fresher commenters say posts like this keep the field feeling alive.

## 💡 Takeaway

A rare full-numbers three-way offer comparison with a clear crowd verdict: Booking over Oracle Health despite Oracle's higher first-year package.
