# Free no-refusal models for bug bounty: MiniMax M3.1 Flash and nodecode Cyber v1

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/ide9x/status/2119986387638497519)** · `DestroyerX` · 2026-11-10 · 24 replies · 76 reposts · 2K likes · 1K bookmarks · 83.4K views

## 💬 Essence

Two posts about free models for bug bounty work. IDE9X recommends MiniMax M3.1 Flash, now free in OpenCode, and says Flash finds bugs that DeepSeek misses. His order is DeepSeek v4.1 Flash, then MiniMax M2.6 Flash. Replies name GLM 5.3 abliterated as another favourite, and one asks about a rate limit after 10 minutes. The second post, by painn_x, announces nodecode Cyber v1, an abliterated DeepSeek v4.1 Flash with no refusals, 1M context (262K on the free tier), EU hosting and no stored prompts. To get a free key you create an account, pay $2 to unlock the paid tier as a bot filter, grab a key and run nodecode login. The free tier was limited in time and the author expected it to end around 9 Nov, so it has probably ended. Replies say a $2 top-up is not free, and one paid and saw no free option.

## 💡 Takeaway

Use no-refusal models only for authorised testing. Treat the nodecode claims about privacy and price as unverified.
