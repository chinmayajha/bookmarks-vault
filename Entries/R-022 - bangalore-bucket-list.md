## 💬 Essence

A visitor from Portland asks what he shouldn't miss in a week of work travel, and 136 replies assemble a crowd-sourced Bangalore itinerary. The top answer (118 upvotes) lists Lalbagh Botanical Garden, Cubbon Park, Vidhana Soudha, Bangalore Palace, Church Street/MG Road nightlife and a pre-6am Nandi Hills sunrise; others add Chitrakala Parishat, Jayanagar and Basavanagudi for the authentic local experience, Vidyarthi Bhavan masala dose and filter coffee, CTR for breakfast over MTR's rush hour, Donne biryani, a Malleshwaram walk, a sunset climb at Savandurga, drinks at The Reservoir, and a museum crawl (MAP, NGMA, Government Museum, Visvesvaraya, Nehru Planetarium). OP engages throughout, confirming he already saw Lalbagh and loved Jayanagar.
