# Maritime (YC F26) hosts each AI agent on its own computer for $1/month

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/mariagorskikh/status/2108632744838488072)** · `Maria Gorskikh` · 2026-10-09 · 115 replies · 90 reposts · 568 likes · 394 bookmarks · 44K views · 🎬 · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Maria Gorskikh launched Maritime, a YC F26 company giving startups infrastructure to deploy, manage and scale agents, each with its own computer, starting at $1 per agent per month. The thread keeps the math deliberately simple - 500 agents is $500 - so founders can reason about cost without a spreadsheet. Early users reply that they have already set it up and that it is special, while the sharper questions are about what the bill looks like at real scale and whether the launch video's people were agents or founders. Five days later the same company used the platform to demo a persistent Android phone for shopping on Amazon after Amazon blocked AI assistants, which is a more concrete illustration of the 'each agent gets a computer' thesis than the launch copy.

## 💡 Takeaway

A clean per-agent pricing model for agent infrastructure; the Amazon Android demo is the proof that persistent, logged-in computers are the actual product.
