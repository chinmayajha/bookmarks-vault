# If someone bails from a 5-way Airbnb, do they get their money back?

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/darrelltalksfi/status/2048034972846653917)** · `Darrell Aden` · 2026-04-25 · 318 replies · 124 reposts · 4.1K likes · 2.1M views · 183 bookmarks

## 💬 Essence

Darrell asks whether a person who split an Airbnb 5 ways and bails last minute (sick, personal reasons) should get a refund or eat it as a sunk cost; his thread notes people feel some type of way. Replies coalesce around sunk cost - you committed and others budgeted around you - with nuance: the bailing person should still offer to pay while friends overrule and cover them ('a battle of generosity'), flake vs family death changes the answer, and one says you pay for what you agreed to; 4,120 likes and 2.06M views.

## 💡 Takeaway

A viral money-etiquette debate with a clear emerging consensus norm - handy for group-trip and fairness product angles.
