# This is the worst anti-dandruff shampoo, apparently

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/CoconutShawarma/status/1983469253656469562)** · `Ramen 🍉` · 2025-10-28 · 87 replies · 1.2K likes · 596.2K views

## 💬 Essence

Quote-tweeting his own challenge to show a better anti-dandruff shampoo that actually works, OP declares the pictured one the worst; his self-thread asks followers whether it works and claims bar soap works better. Replies tell him to ask his dermatologist, one reports a similar formula (Ketomac), another is confused because they use that exact product, and the most useful reply explains that ketoconazole treats seborrheic dermatitis, selenium sulfide treats oily mild dandruff and climbazol is for something else - there is no one-for-all solution.

## 💡 Takeaway

Light haircare thread, but the active-ingredients distinction in replies is genuinely useful.
