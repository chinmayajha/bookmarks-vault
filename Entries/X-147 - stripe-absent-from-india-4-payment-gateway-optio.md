# Stripe absent from India? 4 payment gateway options

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/frog_omo/status/1920748996273865031)** · `Amit | Frogomo | AI 🐸` · 2025-05-09 · 17 replies · 227 likes · 17K views

## 💬 Essence

Answering a reply that asks what to use for Indian and global users since Stripe isn't available in India, OP compares Razorpay (best local option, 98.2% uptime, solid dashboard), DodoPayments (underrated for international-to-India, quoted at 1.9% + Rs3), PayU (enterprise-grade but clunky API) and Cashfree (decent for recurring billing), and says his costliest mistake was building payment logic into the core app. In the follow-up he admits he misquoted DodoPayments' rate - it actually charges 4% + 40 cents - which two replies independently correct, while others ask about Paddle and whether Razorpay works outside India.

## 💡 Takeaway

Handy India gateway shortlist; the fee correction in-thread is a good reminder to verify pricing claims.
