# Backlink research prompt: 30 verified free opportunities and a 7-day plan

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/2whazzuup/status/2113470379713085739)** · `Grant O` · 2026-10-23 · 9 replies · 15 reposts · 165 likes · 588 bookmarks · 25.9K views

## 💬 Essence

Grant posts a reusable prompt. The model acts as an SEO link-building researcher; you fill in your website, niche, audience and country. It must find 30 legitimate free backlink opportunities in categories such as niche directories, industry resource pages, free listings, community and association directories and free tool directories. For each one it gives the site name, direct submission URL, relevance, whether it is free, how to get the link, approval needs and effort. Rules: verify each site exists and accepts submissions, exclude guest posts, paid placements and link farms, do not invent URLs, and return fewer if fewer qualify. It then ranks the results and writes a 7-day acquisition plan. A self-reply sells a Gumroad prompt library with 400+ backlink opportunities. A reply warns that the model will still invent some links, so click every one before you submit.

## 💡 Takeaway

A solid prompt skeleton with good anti-hallucination rules. Always verify every URL by hand.
