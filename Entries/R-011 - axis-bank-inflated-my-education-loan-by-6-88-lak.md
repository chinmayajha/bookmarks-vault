# Axis Bank inflated my education loan by ₹6.88 lakhs overnight during complaint

**[Open original ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/CreditCardsIndia/comments/1sscc7m/axis_bank_inflated_my_education_loan_by_688_lakhs/)** · `u/davidben09` · 2026-04-22 · ↑410 · 34 comments · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP documents a ₹25 lakh Axis education loan where 54 EMIs were paid with zero overdue, yet on foreclosure the branch quoted ₹6.88 lakhs more after a 27.02.2026 batch operation re-booked all instalments back to 2022, added 50+ offsetting entries, jumped outstanding from ₹13,08,775 to ₹19,97,037 and silently extended tenure from 138 to 158 months — all while his complaint was open. Replies confirm a pattern: PNB and UBI borrowers describe identical education-loan misreporting that was resolved only after RBI Lokpal complaints, one says 'Axis is well known for crap like this', and others hand him a concrete escalation path (ombudsman, appellate authority, specific RBI email IDs like cgmicdosco@rbi.org.in and aaos@rbi.org.in) plus advice to document every damage including mental anguish. Sentiment is furious solidarity, 410 upvotes.
