# Dividends taxed at 52% vs capital gains 14.95% - the VC arbitrage

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/Nithin0dha/status/1985316723533435191)** · `Nithin Kamath` · 2025-11-03 · 279 replies · 918 reposts · 5.9K likes · 1.2M views · 3K bookmarks · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Nithin Kamath lays out the Indian tax asymmetry: taking money out of a business as dividends costs an effective 52% (25% corporate tax + 35.5% personal income) versus 14.95% through capital gains - so investors minimize corporate tax by showing minimal profits or losses, burn cash to build a growth narrative, then sell shares at a higher valuation while paying far less tax (spending also makes it harder for competitors to survive; India's R&D is 0.7% of GDP). He argues VCs are essentially playing a tax arbitrage game, which is why so few VC-backed listings show profit. Replies push back - Ashneer Grover says by that logic all investors should hold for dividends instead of selling, others summarize 'VC math: 52% on profits, ~15% on gains, so they'd rather show losses', and one quotes 'your margin is my opportunity'; 5,861 likes, 2,991 bookmarks and 1.17M views.

## 💡 Takeaway

The clearest explanation of why Indian startups treat profit as optional - essential context for IPO investing.
