# HyperFrames claims its reverse-any-video skill is 20x faster than REA-Video

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/Rames_Jusso/status/2122465287354409304)** · `James Russo` · 2026-11-09 · 67 replies · 188 reposts · 3.2K likes · 5.4K bookmarks · 153.2K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

Rames Jusso of HyperFrames compares its reverse-any-video skill with REA-Video, the skill in the quoted Riley Brown post, where Claude downloads a YouTube video and analyses every frame to extract the editing style. The post claims 81 seconds against 1,595 seconds, about 20x faster, and a similarity score (SSIM) of 0.96 against 0.78. Install with npx skills add heygen-com/hyperframes. Replies in the thread say it works best with Opus 5.5 or Fable 5.2 and works inside Grok bot. Other readers ask for the repo link, which model to use, and whether 8 GB of RAM is enough. This is the vendor's own benchmark. See X-254.

## 💡 Takeaway

A useful pair of skills to compare. Run both on one video before you trust the benchmark.
