PIEDS at BITS Pilani opens applications for Udaan, a mentor-driven launchpad for early-stage student startups: a 2-day offline bootcamp plus 8 weeks of virtual mentorship for 30 startups, with the top 10 going to a June demo day to pitch investors across India.
