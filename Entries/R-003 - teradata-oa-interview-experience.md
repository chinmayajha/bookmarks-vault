## 💬 Essence

OP writes the finale of his campus-placement series: after rejections from Google, Oracle, Amazon, Samsung and Siemens, Teradata — hiring from his college for the first time and normally an IIT-only recruiter — came through in Week 3 with an OA and interviews that leaned on core CS and projects rather than hard DSA. The 109 replies turn celebratory: readers say they'd been following the daily 'episodes', congratulate him, ask how the internship went and whether they can DM, while a couple of technical comments dig into how to structure the class-based solution he was asked. The thread's dominant sentiment is warm and motivational, with OP insisting his success was luck as much as skill.
