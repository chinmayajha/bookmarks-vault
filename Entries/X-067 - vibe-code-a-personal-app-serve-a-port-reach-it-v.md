# Vibe-code a personal app, serve a port, reach it via Tailscale

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/linguinelabs/status/2029242752987517018)** · `Kevin` · 2026-03-04 · 55 replies · 138 reposts · 3.3K likes · 231.2K views · 3.3K bookmarks · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Kevin argues it's pretty insane that he can vibe-code any web app (acne tracker, gym tracker), run it on a port of his laptop and access it on his phone via Tailscale - no domain or app store, all free, completely tailored to him. His thread says storage is SQLite (fine for tracking apps), that he's nearly out of Vercel free quota and will try Railway for his next project with users. Replies shrug that auth doesn't matter for one user ('who cares, algo'), call personal apps a fun playground, others say they deploy on Railway with a Claude skill plus better_auth in about 10 minutes, and a Tailscale person invites DMs; 3,289 likes and 3,322 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

A popular no-deploy personal-app workflow (local port + Tailscale + SQLite) - real signal for local-first tooling.
