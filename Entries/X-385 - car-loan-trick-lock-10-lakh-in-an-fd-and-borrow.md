# Car-loan trick: lock 10 lakh in an FD and borrow 9 lakh as an overdraft instead

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/Mr_Vivekji/status/2113100715476516881)** · `VIVEK` · 2026-10-21 · 52 replies · 53 reposts · 435 likes · 682 bookmarks · 73.8K views

## 💬 Essence

Vivek argues that if you have 10 lakh in the bank you should not take a car loan. Keep the 10 lakh in an FD for 7 years at 6%, take a 9 lakh overdraft at 7%, and buy the car. His figures: FD maturity 15.17 lakh rupees, overdraft EMI 13,583 for 84 months, interest earned 5.17 lakh, interest paid 2.41 lakh, total gain 2.76 lakh. He says car loans carry higher rates and processing fees, while an overdraft costs about 1% more than the FD rate with no processing charge. The post quotes a debate on whether to take a loan when you can pay cash. Replies push back: you lose the liquidity that is the point of an FD, you pay interest on your own money while the FD interest is taxed, and many people lack the cash to cover EMIs. One reply says it only helps if you know the structure.

## 💡 Takeaway

An interesting structure with real trade-offs: liquidity, tax on FD interest and a modest gain. Check the numbers with your own bank.
