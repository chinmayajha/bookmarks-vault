# Core CS and C++ depth required for quant dev roles is unmatched

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/PriyanshuP1405/status/1987865318866481476)** · `Priyanshu Priyank` · 2025-11-10 · 30 replies · 111 reposts · 1.9K likes · 91K views · 1K bookmarks

## 💬 Essence

Priyanshu argues the level of core CS and C++ knowledge required for quant dev roles is unmatched. His thread concedes not everyone is taught this way and that writing compilers yourself may not ensure you ace quant dev interviews - candidates are grilled harder and need to be perfect across traits, and not every curriculum goes deep. Replies give examples (refcounting as intuitive OS design), say write a language or compiler once and you'll understand it all, argue most curricula do have compiler and OS classes, prefer a breadth-first cover of OS internals over 'learning linux', and one says it doesn't match their experience at all; 1,950 likes and 1,003 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

A reality check on the quant dev hiring bar - the systems depth (OS internals, compilers, C++) expected.
