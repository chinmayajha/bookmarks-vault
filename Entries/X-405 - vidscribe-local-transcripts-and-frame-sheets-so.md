# vidscribe: local transcripts and frame sheets so agents can watch videos

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/samuelxzhang/status/2122058913692746224)** · `Samuel Zhang` · 2026-11-15 · 36 replies · 68 reposts · 1.3K likes · 1.5K bookmarks · 46.2K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

Samuel Zhang releases /vidscribe, which lets agents watch videos. It uses ffmpeg and adds a local transcript, 16 frames per image and an index that ties each frame to what was said, so the agent knows where to look. He says it saves tokens and keeps accuracy in his eval. It runs on Apple Silicon only, is local and free, and works with any agent. He says Gemini hears music and sounds and vidscribe does not, and he has not run a head-to-head test. Replies ask why it is better than what agents do natively with ffmpeg and why it is Apple-only. His answer is that he uses a Mac. His thread also links OpenFilm. See X-380.

## 💡 Takeaway

Useful when an agent must reason over a screen recording or tutorial video. It has no audio understanding beyond speech.
