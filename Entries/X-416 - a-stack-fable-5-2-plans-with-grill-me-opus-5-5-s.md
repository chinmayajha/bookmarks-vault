# A stack: Fable 5.2 plans with /grill-me, Opus 5.5 subagents build, Cloudflare CLI hosts

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/EXM7777/status/2120856056977113363)** · `Machina` · 2026-11-12 · 46 replies · 45 reposts · 1.3K likes · 1.5K bookmarks · 62.3K views

## 💬 Essence

Machina shares a compact stack. Fable 5.2 on high effort runs /grill-me, then /to-spec and /to-tickets. Opus 5.5 subagents on medium effort run /implement. The Cloudflare CLI handles hosting and the database. Replies say the Cloudflare CLI is the real insider choice. One adds a deep-opinion skill and a delegation skill in Fable 5.2. Several readers say grill-me before the spec is the part they will copy, because they used to skip straight to building. One asks whether the grill step catches real problems or only slows work.

## 💡 Takeaway

A good template for splitting plan and build across two models. Test whether the grill step pays off on your tasks.
