# A free edu-style email at mail.flame.edu.kg, with an invite code: treat with care

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/Nico69Oliveira/status/2119347566316019958)** · `Nico爱分享` · 2026-11-08 · 80 replies · 156 reposts · 2.7K likes · 5.5K bookmarks · 233K views

## 💬 Essence

Nico posts mail.flame.edu.kg, which offers a free student-style email address valid until 2083 with no student ID. The first invite code was Happy November 11th. Later posts in the thread give new codes (Snowden, FREESnowden) and admit the domain is a Kyrgyzstan education domain. Replies are mixed. One user got an address, but a site did not accept it as a university address. Others say the invite code expired, or ask how to use it for education benefits when a form asks for a university name. This looks like a way around student verification, and many programs ban such use.

## 💡 Takeaway

Do not rely on it for student perks, because services check eligibility and can close accounts. Use a real student status.
