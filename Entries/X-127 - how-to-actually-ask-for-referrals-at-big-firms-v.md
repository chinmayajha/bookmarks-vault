# How to actually ask for referrals at big firms vs startups

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/a_hahahahad/status/1969738332604154061)** · `Ahad 🥑` · 2025-09-21 · 21 replies · 1.4K likes · 87.5K views

## 💬 Essence

OP lays out a referral playbook: for MNCs, pull job IDs from the careers page and email employees with just job IDs + resume (nothing extra); for startups, cold DM or email with your CV, why you're relevant, and proof of work (portfolio, GitHub, past projects). In the self-thread he adds that a referral means your resume is directly reviewed by a hiring manager instead of being filtered by an automated system, and that the approach varies by company. Replies add a concrete upgrade - name something specific from the recipient's recent posts/projects so it doesn't read as mass spam - plus questions about how much a referral actually raises your odds and whether game theory is used in quant roles.

## 💡 Takeaway

Split-by-company-type referral script; the 'reference their recent work' reply is the part that makes it work.
