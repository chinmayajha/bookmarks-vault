# A Codex-built whey price bot found a 2 kg deal the apps buried

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/unk_data/status/2110661566031172042)** · `Vijay` · 2026-10-15 · 9 replies · 2 reposts · 189 likes · 80 bookmarks · 10.3K views

## 💬 Essence

Vijay asked Codex to reverse engineer an Android quick-commerce price-comparison app, pulled its backend endpoints with the REA skill, and built a whey-protein price-alert bot. It paid off immediately: 2 kg for ₹3,993 on Instamart when the going rate was ₹3,200+ for 1 kg. He says the bot polls twice a day, that proxies are the real operational headache because sites block scrapers, and that the whole thing is easy enough to vibe-code now. Replies ask for the repo, the polling rate and which whey; the more useful skepticism is that reverse-engineering works when the target API is clean but falls apart on messy DOMs, and that IP/account blocking is the part nobody demos.

## 💡 Takeaway

A small, concrete example of agent-built scraping economics - the win is the endpoint discovery, the hidden cost is proxies and anti-bot maintenance.
