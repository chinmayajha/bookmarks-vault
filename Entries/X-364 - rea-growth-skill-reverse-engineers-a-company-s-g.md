## 💬 Essence

Jason Zhou introduces /REA-growth, an open-source skill that reverse-engineers any company's growth: name a company and get a growth blueprint with every SEO, AEO, ads and social move on a timeline. A full teardown of Lovable costs 0.58 dollars. It runs on the treg platform (treg.to/rea-growth), made with a partner, and the repo is superdesigndev/treg. In replies, one user ran it on Cursor for 0.48 dollars and shared the report page, and others say it is a smart way to study competitor growth. One reply wonders whether consultants are doomed, and another doubts it is as good as claimed. See the earlier REA and treg entries.

## 💡 Takeaway

A cheap competitor-growth report generator. Spot-check one report you already know before trusting it.
