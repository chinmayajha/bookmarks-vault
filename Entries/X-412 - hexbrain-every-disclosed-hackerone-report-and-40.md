# HexBrain: every disclosed HackerOne report and 405K CVEs on one interactive map

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/hackerspider1/status/2121200814387798381)** · `Shubham Gupta 🇮🇳` · 2026-11-13 · 76 replies · 194 reposts · 1K likes · 1.1K bookmarks · 43.7K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

Shubham Gupta builds HexBrain, an interactive map of 15,000+ publicly disclosed HackerOne reports grouped by vulnerability class, weakness and program, with full write-ups on how each bug was found. It also lists 405,000+ CVEs with exploited ones in red, plus bypass and payload libraries. The site says you can connect Claude, ChatGPT or another AI assistant to search it. In the thread he says access is by invitation (DM an email), the map shows what each program accepted, and an MCP exists but free public access costs too much. Replies ask for report status (valid, N/A, informative), a trend over the years, and a comparison of prerequisites between similar findings.

## 💡 Takeaway

A strong research aid for authorised bug bounty work. Access is by invitation for now.
