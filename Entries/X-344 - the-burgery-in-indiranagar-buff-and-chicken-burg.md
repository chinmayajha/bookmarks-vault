# The Burgery In, Indiranagar: buff and chicken burgers worth the trip

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/ItsAllDandy_/status/2005921572612366697)** · `In her sandwich-making era` · 2025-12-30 · 33 replies · 24 reposts · 1.4K likes · 566 bookmarks · 91.2K views

## 💬 Essence

A straight-up Bangalore burger recommendation: The Burgery In in Indiranagar does buff and chicken burgers, really crispy fries and a peanut butter shake that the poster calls soooo flavorful. In the thread she narrows it to the Ultimate and Frizzled burgers - the Frizzled has a melted crunchy layer of cheese - and says she liked it better than Truffles. Replies are the useful part: one compares it with Smash Guys (good burger, excellent bone-marrow side), another recommends Guerrilla Diner, and a third went for a birthday dinner and called it the best burger of their life. It is a low-stakes bookmark, but a well-validated one.

## 💡 Takeaway

A concrete Indiranagar burger pick with enough reply confirmation to trust; go for the Frizzled and the peanut butter shake.
