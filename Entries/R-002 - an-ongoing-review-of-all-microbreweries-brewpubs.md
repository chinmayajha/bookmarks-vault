# An ongoing review of all Microbreweries/ Brewpubs in Gurgaon

**[Open original ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/gurgaon/comments/t3fdbe/an_ongoing_review_of_all_microbreweries_brewpubs/)** · `u/QuickOriginal` · 2022-02-28 · ↑80 · 33 comments · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP maintains a running, self-imposed-serious review of every Gurgaon microbrewery — visiting in the afternoon, trying dark beer before wheat, rating beer, food, ambience, service and price holistically, with the sheet last updated 30 October 2025 and framed as his 'life's work'. The 33 replies are almost entirely affectionate praise ('Doing God's work', 'you sir are a fine gentleman') plus spot recommendations: Big Pitcher and Clock Tower called out as strong, others saying it pushed them to try the places they'd skipped. Many commenters saved the post as a reference, with the overall sentiment that this is the definitive Gurgaon beer guide.

## 💡 Takeaway

A long-lived, methodically updated crowd reference for Gurgaon craft-beer spots — the kind of evergreen local guide worth keeping bookmarked for recommendations.
