# Axis Magnus: how to extract 15-20% rewards

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/AashishPunjabi1/status/1619953735777804290)** · `Aashish Punjabi` · 2023-01-30 · 83 replies · 1.5K likes · 415.7K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP, unpaid, makes the case for the Axis Magnus card: it offers up to 15-20% rewards on spending and suits anyone earning over Rs18L as a primary card; the Rs10,000 + GST fee is offset by a Rs10K TataCliq voucher or domestic flight (he recommends the voucher), so you effectively pay GST only; ideal spend is Rs12-15L a year, though Rs5-6L including rent works. He does the math - 1 Edge Reward point per Rs16.66 means about 30,000 ER on Rs5L, convertible at 5:4 into 24,000 Vistara/SpiceJet miles (a BLR-BOM flight costs 4,500 CV points versus Rs4,500 cash fares) or Marriott Bonvoy - and shows how to max it: rent, groceries, bills and daily spend on the card (Swiggy, Amazon, instead of UPI) plus Gyftr vouchers, with monthly reward caps.
