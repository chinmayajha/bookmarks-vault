# Design.how ships a copy-paste design system for AI apps and dev tools

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/arlanmarat/status/2110425467996209244)** · `Arlan Marat` · 2026-10-14 · 23 replies · 9 reposts · 341 likes · 331 bookmarks · 13.1K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

Arlan Marat published a new design system aimed specifically at AI apps and developer tools, with copyable code at design.how/systems. The post is deliberately minimal - a link and 'new design system' - but the replies show the niche it fills: people are tired of agents 'creatively reinterpreting' a palette into six shades of purple, and a constrained system gives them something concrete to build against. Marat says his own agent came up with it, which is either a useful signal about agent-generated design systems or a warning about how little human curation is behind it, depending on your priors.

## 💡 Takeaway

A lightweight, copy-paste starting point for AI-product UI; the value is constraint and speed, not originality.
