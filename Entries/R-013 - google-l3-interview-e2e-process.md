# Google L3 Interview E2E Process

**[Open original ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/leetcode/comments/1s8nxsp/google_l3_interview_e2e_process/)** · `u/whitew4k3r` · 2026-03-31 · ↑14 · 8 comments

## 💬 Essence

OP walks through his Google L3 loop in India: a referral for Ads wasn't shortlisted, but a recruiter proactively reached out for Google Cloud, asked about prep and gave him two weeks; round 1 was a heap/top-K-with-duplicates design question where he had to go from a two-heap brute force to a vectors-only solution before ultimately being rejected four days after the onsite. Replies clarify the logistics — India location, general SWE role, one week between virtual and onsite, rejection call/email four days later — and ask what he felt he lacked; OP says he prepared 2-3 weeks and believes a small implementation mistake sank him. The thread's takeaway is that even a clean-looking loop can end over minor implementation sloppiness.

## 💡 Takeaway

A realistic end-to-end Google L3 timeline (recruiter outreach → 2 weeks prep → loop → rejection in 4 days) and the reminder that implementation precision decides outcomes.
