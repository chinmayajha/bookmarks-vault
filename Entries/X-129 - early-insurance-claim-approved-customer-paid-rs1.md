# Early insurance claim approved: customer paid Rs1 out of pocket

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/NIKHILLJHA/status/1966794622593851634)** · `Nikhil Jha` · 2025-09-13 · 71 replies · 670 likes · 68.6K views

## 💬 Essence

OP recounts a successful early claim: a customer bought a Rs25L Niva Bupa policy in March 2025, then needed a nerve-ballooning procedure; early claims are normally rejected and pushed to reimbursement, but his team escalated to top management, who reviewed and accepted it, so the customer paid just Re1. His self-thread says his firm has built insurer claim-support channels for exactly this and that companies don't give cashless in early claims. Replies split between praise for the advisor and outrage - 'if the claim was genuine why approach top management?' - with commenters arguing social media pressure is what actually moves insurers.

## 💡 Takeaway

Insightful look at how Indian health claims really get paid; the 'why is this not corruption' debate is the value.
