# CLAUDE.md 'two-way doors' rule lets the agent decide alone and stop only at one-way doors

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/daradoescode/status/2117433338319544735)** · `Dara A.` · 2026-11-03 · 4 replies · 1 reposts · 92 likes · 160 bookmarks · 2.9K views

## 💬 Essence

Dara posts an image of a CLAUDE.md section called 'Two-way doors swing freely' and says something like it helps a lot. The image looks like a GitHub-rendered file from the linked d-stack repo (SunkenInTime/d-stack). The rule: trust the agent with whole problems, take the full scope and use as many subagents as needed. Walk through two-way doors and show the result after. Stop at one-way doors. Do not ask if the answer would be a rubber stamp, and run something instead of asking when a run can answer the question. Save questions for taste and product calls, one at a time. Once a one-way door is opened, it stays open for that work, including for subagents, unless the real situation differs from what was approved. Replies say it is too verbose and could be one or two sentences. One reply suggests a short version: make all decisions yourself, save questions in a doc for later, and move on, ideally as a skill.

## 💡 Takeaway

A reusable autonomy rule for CLAUDE.md. Copy the idea, then shorten it to fit your own work.
