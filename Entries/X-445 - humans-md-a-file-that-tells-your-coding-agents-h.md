# HUMANS.md: a file that tells your coding agents how to work with you

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/neerajjj6785/status/2123337246430650809)** · `Neeraj` · 2026-11-19 · 45 replies · 68 reposts · 1,079 likes · 1,926 bookmarks · 192.9K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Neeraj calls humansmd.dev the work of a genius (1,079 likes, 193K views). The site proposes HUMANS.md: one file at ~/.humans/HUMANS.md that your coding agents read at session start. It says how to write to you, when to ask you to decide and when to interrupt you. AGENTS.md is for your codebase and HUMANS.md is for you. The spec is a draft (0.3). Plugins exist for Claude Code, Codex, Devin and OpenCode, and a CLI loads your profile. Replies are mostly generic praise, and one reader asks why the comments are all bots.

## 💡 Takeaway

A per-person counterpart to AGENTS.md. Early draft, but easy to try.
