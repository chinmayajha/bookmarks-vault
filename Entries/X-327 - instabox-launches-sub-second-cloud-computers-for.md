# InstaBox launches sub-second cloud computers for AI agents, then publishes the pricing

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/hanghuang_/status/2110349488200057326)** · `Hang Huang` · 2026-10-14 · 2.7K replies · 338 reposts · 3.3K likes · 3.4K bookmarks · 360.3K … · 🎬 · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Hang Huang launched InstaBox, cloud computers for AI agents: a 4 vCPU / 8 GB machine spun up in under a second, built-in computer use, snapshots for cloning, persistent files/packages/config and public URLs for local ports. The launch was invite-only, with early access including 20 vCPU and 40 GB RAM free, and the reply count - 2,732 - is almost entirely people asking for codes or pitching use cases (Godot games, agent hosting, multi-agent chat rooms). Five days later the same account published the pricing rationale: one free 4 vCPU machine with no usage limit, aggressive oversubscription economics and Cloudflare-style free-tier logic. The two posts together are a rare public view of launch demand turning into a pricing decision.

## 💡 Takeaway

A strong launch signal for agent-native cloud infra; the follow-up pricing post is the more useful artifact because it explains the unit economics behind the free tier.
