# This cold email sequence will never stop working

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/1820883709840515099)** · `Nick Abraham` · 2024-08-06 · 82 replies · 5.4K likes · 1M views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP posts his cold email sequence and, in the self-thread, answers the skeptics who pile on ('spam', 'this doesn't work', 'quick question is spam'): he calls them limiting claims, says his agency has 150+ active clients and 5K monthly users across SaaS companies and sends 1.5M emails a month for clients, asks a founder how many genuinely good (personalised and relevant) cold emails he receives, and insists it works outside your inbox. Replies split hard: one founder says he gets 20 identical-format ones a day and answers none, another says straight to junk - versus 'this is really good', 'goat', and 'going to try it and report back'.

## 💡 Takeaway

The cold-email debate in one thread - the sequence itself plus credible deliverability skepticism.
