# Huashu-art-motion gives Opus 5.5 35 coded art styles plus a film QA agent

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/Voxyz_ai/status/2109690569563746755)** · `Vox` · 2026-10-12 · 34 replies · 217 reposts · 2K likes · 3.6K bookmarks · 228.3K views · 🎬 · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Vox's huashu-art-motion is a skill that lets Opus 5.5 generate animations in 35 art styles by writing code: 17 that work for any subject (Van Gogh, Seurat, Bauhaus, Ghibli watercolor, 8-bit, vaporwave), 18 for art-history time travel (Monet, Klimt, Dalí, Hopper, cave painting, Greek pottery) and 9 explainer styles (3Blue1Brown, keynote, whiteboard, Vox explainer, Kurzgesagt). Each style specifies what actually moves, music is synthesized note-by-note on a BPM grid, and a QA script plus an independent subagent watches the finished film. The delivery script blocks more than 0.3 seconds of blank frames and blue-purple backgrounds over 30% of the film, which it explicitly labels AI slop. Docs are Chinese; the post includes a long English prompt to drive it.

## 💡 Takeaway

A unusually complete motion-generation pipeline - style cards, beat-synced audio, independent QA and anti-slop gates - worth trying if you already generate video with code.
