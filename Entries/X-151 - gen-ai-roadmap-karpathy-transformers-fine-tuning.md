# Gen AI roadmap: Karpathy, transformers, fine-tuning, RAG, agents

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/romitheguru/status/1889866779138109847)** · `Romee Panchal` · 2025-02-13 · 6 replies · 678 likes · 33K views

## 💬 Essence

OP's path for people who already know Python and neural-net basics: go through Karpathy's Zero to Hero series, train a small transformer on a toy dataset with Hugging Face to understand training/tokenization/inference, then fine-tune a pretrained LLM, then move to RAG and agentic systems, using ChatGPT or Claude for project ideas. His self-thread stresses practical skills that solve company problems, notes AI engineers are not data scientists, links DataCamp's fine-tuning tutorial and an LLM handbook, and frames building projects to expose your gaps as the real test. Replies split into two tracks - pure LLM/agent work versus MLE/data-science fundamentals that actually get you hired - and someone asks for resources on DeepSeek RL.

## 💡 Takeaway

Actionable staged GenAI roadmap, with replies clarifying the AI-engineer vs MLE fork.
