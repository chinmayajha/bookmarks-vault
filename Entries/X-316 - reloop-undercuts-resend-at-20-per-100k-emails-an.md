# Reloop undercuts Resend at $20 per 100K emails and adds inbound mail

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/ThatsPranav/status/2110817677778489400)** · `Pranav Patel` · 2026-10-15 · 52 replies · 37 reposts · 603 likes · 783 bookmarks · 70.1K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Pranav Patel launches Reloop, an open-source email platform positioned as a cheaper Resend: 100K emails for $20/month instead of $90, free to 3,000, with an email API, SMTP relay, inboxes for AI agents, automations, deliverability guardrails, bring-your-own-SMTP and inbound email. It is agentic (the whole UI is available to an AI) and self-hostable. The 'I reverse engineered Resend' framing is what the thread fights about: the top reply says vibe-coding a clone is not reverse engineering and that open-source Resend alternatives have existed for years, another says the price matches Resend without its reliability, and others ask how much was him versus a model. Patel answers that the architecture and decisions are his, that Reloop is not an AWS wrapper, and links a PR adding it to the opencoredev email SDK.

## 💡 Takeaway

A credible cheap Resend alternative if deliverability holds up - the launch is also a case study in how 'reverse engineered' framing invites credit and reliability scrutiny.
