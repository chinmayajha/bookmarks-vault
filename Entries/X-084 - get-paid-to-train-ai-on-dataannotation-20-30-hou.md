## 💬 Essence

Timmy describes Data Annotation: you apply, take a little test to make sure you understand the idea, then get paid to train AI, with different projects opening up that you can log in and complete whenever; pay starts at $20/hour and he's seen $30 for certain projects. His thread drops the account link and later reveals the catch - you need a paid VPN and a US eSIM number, text-app numbers don't work.
