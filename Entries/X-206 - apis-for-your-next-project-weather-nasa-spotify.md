## 💬 Essence

OP's thread of APIs for side projects: OpenWeatherMap for real-time weather data (apps, dashboards), NASA for mission images and space data, Spotify for its music library (playlists, recommendations, search), Google Maps for mapping and location data (directions, street view, satellite imagery - 'many unicorns use this API') and Twilio for messaging, voice and video. He invites people to add more so the thread gets richer, offers to list less-known APIs to anyone who reacts with a wave, and plugs his own account; replies are mostly thanks and save-bot tags.

## 💡 Takeaway

Beginner-friendly API grab-bag for demo and portfolio projects.
