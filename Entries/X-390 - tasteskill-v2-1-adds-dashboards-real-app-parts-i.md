# TasteSkill v2.1 adds dashboards, real app parts in landing pages and a frame check

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/LexnLin/status/2112674349132194185)** · `Leon Lin` · 2026-10-20 · 48 replies · 67 reposts · 1.5K likes · 1.6K bookmarks · 57.6K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

Leon releases TasteSkill v2.1, installed with npx skills add Leonxlnx/taste-skill. It now supports dashboards and app screens. Landing pages for software show real, working parts of the app and not fake mockups. A new frame check looks for a missing title on the first screen, uneven margins, random alignment and empty gaps. There are also new motion recipes and more layouts, and one skill now covers everything including redesigns, with the old styles moved to taste-skill-classics and the image skills to imagegen-skills. The launch video was made with Opus 5.5. He says v2.2 is in work and that full app design is not supported yet. Replies praise the fast shipping (v2 was one week earlier). See the earlier TasteSkill v2 entry.

## 💡 Takeaway

A quick follow-up release. Update if you already use TasteSkill, and test the new frame check on a dashboard.
