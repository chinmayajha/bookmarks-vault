# 'Be more creative' prompt makes Opus 5.5 great at frontend

## 💬 Essence

Anthony shares a deceptively simple prompt for Opus 5.5 frontend work - be more creative, add animations and cool graphics that you create with SVGs or whatever, they could even move - saying it genuinely works even without an example. He then plugs his App Store screenshot tool screeniesapp and jokes about posting ~10 times a day. Replies confirm it worked ('just tried it ... surprisingly its working'), others joke clients have been giving him that prompt for five years, and one applies it to their own site; 1,331 likes with 2,192 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

Evidence that plain-language 'be creative' instructions unlock model frontend quality - cheap to test in your own stack.
