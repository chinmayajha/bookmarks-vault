Ahad Shams says he open-sourced a creative agency inside Claude: 30 skills in one folder. The roles are strategist (research, angles, briefs), copywriter (hooks, primary text, UGC scripts), designer (statics, carousels, variants), UGC director (creator briefs, shot lists, AI UGC), media buyer (setup, budgets, testing, scaling) and analyst (reports, fatigue, next tests). Each agent hands off to the next, from brief to hooks to ads to report, then the loop restarts. You can run it with your own team or pair it with HeyOz to turn briefs into finished ads.
