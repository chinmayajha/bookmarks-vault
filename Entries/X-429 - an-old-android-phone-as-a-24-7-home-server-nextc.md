# An old Android phone as a 24/7 home server: Nextcloud, Pi-hole and Navidrome at 2 to 3 W

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/0x0SojalSec/status/2119100186874306652)** · `Md Ismail Šojal 🕷️` · 2026-11-07 · 57 replies · 176 reposts · 2.7K likes · 2.5K bookmarks · 154.7K views

## 💬 Essence

Md Ismail Sojal turned a cheap old Android phone into a home server that runs Nextcloud, Pi-hole, Navidrome music streaming and a live status dashboard. It draws 2 to 3 W on battery with the screen off, using a Debian 13 chroot and Magisk, with no extra hardware. His thread links a Reddit homelab post about a similar Xiaomi Redmi 10C build. Replies raise battery risk. One says to cap charge at 80%, and another had a swollen battery after 2 weeks plugged in. One reply says to drop Android for mainline Linux with postmarketOS or Alpine and links a Kubernetes cluster write-up. Others ask about external storage and the dashboard app.

## 💡 Takeaway

A fun low-power project. Limit the battery charge or remove the battery before you run it 24/7.
