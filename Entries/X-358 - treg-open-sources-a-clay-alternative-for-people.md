## 💬 Essence

Treg announces an open-source 'Clay killer': 85% cheaper than Clay, the most accurate on a people-search benchmark, 25x faster, and runnable inside Claude Code or Codex for go-to-market work. A demo video and the try-it page (treg.to/people-search) come with it, and a self-reply links the repo superdesigndev/treg, described as OpenRouter for agent tools. Replies are friendly: people like having one key for many data tools and say it is easier than building another Clay table. One reply notes the product is powered by Jev.
