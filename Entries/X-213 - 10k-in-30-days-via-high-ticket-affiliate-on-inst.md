# $10K in 30 days via high-ticket affiliate on Instagram

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/jeremynickmoser/status/1570079944092295170)** · `Jeremy Moser` · 2022-09-14 · 107 replies · 3.3K likes · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP's plan to make $10K in 30 days on Instagram from $0 (no followers, skills, clients or connections) is high-ticket affiliate marketing with a twist: he claims to have tried 20+ online money-makers before building a 7-figure business. The method: pick an influencer whose $1k+ course sells well (or a niche with many skilled creators like Airbnb), secure a 25-50% affiliate cut by DM or email (each $1K sale = $250+), then either run a *name*clips/*name*moments account cutting their most valuable or controversial bits into short-form clips (with permission) positioning their vehicle as the right one, or build a theme page like 'airbnbauthority' repurposing the 50-100 best-performing pieces 2-3x a day. He frames it as the tweaked, sustainable version of what Tate did with organic content. No reply digest was captured beyond ten conversation tweets.

Detailed theme-page affiliate funnel - specific enough mechanics to test, with familiar hype framing.
