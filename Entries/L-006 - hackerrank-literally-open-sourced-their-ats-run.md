# HackerRank literally open-sourced their ATS — run your resume against it

**[Open original ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7467174430838968320)** · `Dhananjay Aggarwal` · 2026-06-01 · ♥1,681 · 78 comments · 45 reposts · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

After a rejection, Dhananjay fired a frustrated email at HackerRank asking what they actually look for — and co-founder/CTO Harishankaran Karunanidhi replied personally, not to explain the rejection but to have him run his resume through their ATS, whose hiring agent is fully open-sourced on GitHub. The post's thesis: a decent CGPA, a todo app and a LeetCode streak no longer get you through, because companies run AI agents over resumes and you can now audit your own resume against that agent. The 1,681 reactions and 45 reposts make it the highest-engagement item here; comments include the repo link, a mild rebuke for exposing email initials, requests for his resume, and one user reporting the local Ollama/Gemma run is extremely slow — to which OP replies 'ollama sucks'.

## 💡 Takeaway

The most actionable ATS insight in this set: HackerRank's actual scoring agent is public, so you can test your resume against the real thing instead of guessing.
