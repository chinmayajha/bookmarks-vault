OP posts a nine-item annual challenge - no alcohol, 4-6 eggs a day, 3 litres of water, 7-8 hours sleep, 10,000+ steps, 50+ push-ups daily, no sugar at breakfast, 1,000 words written daily, and five gratitude items - and in the self-thread jokes 'sorry to break this to you, but I eat 9 per day and I am fit'.
