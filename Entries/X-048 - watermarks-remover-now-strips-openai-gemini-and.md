## 💬 Essence

Guillaume Meyer updates watermarks-remover to support watermarks from OpenAI and Gemini in addition to Claude. His thread is candid: the repo currently only removes metadata from supported file formats, removing steganographic marks MAY come later, and he questions whether watermarking is right at all - replies from Eli Gaultney push on whether it includes indirect PII or provider info. Comments argue AI watermarking was always a compliance checkbox rather than a defense, one says it should be in all harnesses, another jokes the tool deserves a $900B valuation; 11,412 likes, 23,589 bookmarks and 2.4M views.

## 💡 Takeaway

The most-saved privacy/provenance tool in this batch - hard evidence of how little users trust AI watermarking.
