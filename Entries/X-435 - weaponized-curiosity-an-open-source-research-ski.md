## 💬 Essence

Ayush Codes turned a research method into an open-source skill for Codex and Claude Code. The post quotes Archie Sengupta, who describes the method: have a strong model run a very large number of web searches, then think from first principles using research findings, failures, tech blogs and articles from a recent window. The repo is ayushrajsinghparihar/weaponized-curiosity, and a listing is on innernet.live. He asks people to star it, fork it and open issues. Replies are short praise. One reader plans to extend it with their own tool suite.
