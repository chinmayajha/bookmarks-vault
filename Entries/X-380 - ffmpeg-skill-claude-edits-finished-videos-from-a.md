# ffmpeg-skill: Claude edits finished videos from a folder with one prompt

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/guilhermebrasil/status/2113751145160061438)** · `guilherme` · 2026-10-23 · 44 replies · 188 reposts · 2.9K likes · 5K bookmarks · 156.1K views

## 💬 Essence

Guilherme answers his own question about a video editing skill for Claude with ffmpeg-skill (kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill). Claude watches the video and edits it in high quality and fast, and he calls it the best he found for editing ready footage. In the thread he put several interview videos in a folder with the skill, described the result, and Claude joined them, added image effects, captions with correct timing and transitions. All edits are done by prompt. For simple edits with cuts, effects and captions it is enough; for motion graphics use Remotion, Brag or Hyperframe. Replies share alternatives such as SaneVideo, video-bitedit and heditor, and ask whether fine-tuning is possible or whether editing is prompt-only. See the related Opus video and levelsio entries.

## 💡 Takeaway

A practical skill for cuts, captions and transitions. For animated graphics, pair it with Remotion.
