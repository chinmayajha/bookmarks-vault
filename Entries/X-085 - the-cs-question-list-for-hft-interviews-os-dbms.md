# The CS question list for HFT interviews: OS, DBMS, networks

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/pulkit_mittal_/status/2012921301414162684)** · `pulkit mittal` · 2026-01-18 · 10 replies · 67 reposts · 720 likes · 78.1K views · 1.4K bookmarks · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Pulkit says he had to prepare this computer science round even for a senior HFT role, and all companies ask these questions of freshers: OS (process vs thread, virtual memory/paging/thrashing, fragmentation, semaphore vs mutex, producer-consumer, deadlock and Banker's algorithm, scheduling, RAID, mapping), DBMS (ACID, normalization, conflict serializability, concurrency protocols, locks, indexing, vertical vs horizontal scaling), Computer Networks (TCP vs UDP, types of network delays, ...). His self-thread adds that when he got the HFT offer the hardest interview was multithreading - even more important for seniors - with the LeetCode concurrency list, and replies ask whether HFTs want only C++ and Python and say CP matters most; 720 likes and 1,357 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

The canonical HFT/fintech CS fundamentals checklist - heavily saved by interview preppers.
