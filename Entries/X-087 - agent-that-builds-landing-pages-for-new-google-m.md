# Agent that builds landing pages for new Google Maps businesses

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/kuberdenis/status/2015721648293372067)** · `Denislav Gavrilov` · 2026-01-26 · 27 replies · 68 reposts · 1.9K likes · 149.9K views · 2.2K bookmarks · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Denislav floats a business idea: an agent that checks newly registered Google Maps businesses daily, finds their email, creates 20 variations of a static landing page (10 Claude Code, 10 Codex), hosts them on a company subdomain and emails them to the owner. His thread says he won't do it, but his friend got a cold email from an SMB asking 400 EUR/year for an awful static website, so he claude-coded 10 variations, hosted on Cloudflare Pages with a GitHub agent making changes from labeled issues and auto-deploying previews - 'she's now a proud website owner'. Replies split: someone's already building it, email deliverability will be brutal, one calls it a horrible idea, others reframe it as becoming the default first website for every new business with a claim-to-own flow and affiliate hosting; 1,850 likes and 2,194 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

A viral, morally murky automation-agency idea - the reply debate maps the go-to-market and deliverability walls.
