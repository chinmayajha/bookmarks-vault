# 4 marketing strategies with 1 product, 0 audience, $0

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/marclou/status/1787501337699315768)** · `Marc Lou` · 2024-05-06 · 55 replies · 1.5K likes · 330.7K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP lays out the four channels he uses to get customers with no audience and no budget: (1) launch platforms - Product Hunt, Reddit, Hacker News - where the headline is 80% of a successful launch and AI directories are worth listing on; (2) cold outreach - which got him his first dollar online and a SaaS to $4,000 MRR - requiring personalised, benefit-oriented, five-sentence-max messages and working best for high-ticket or B2B, with niche newsletters as outreach at scale; (3) building an audience - pick the platform matching your medium, build cool stuff, learn the platform's code from successful creators, respect people's time, and find your signature; the fourth is cut off in the capture. He also notes he doesn't do sponsorships.

## 💡 Takeaway

Evergreen zero-budget GTM playbook - useful as a launch checklist skeleton.
