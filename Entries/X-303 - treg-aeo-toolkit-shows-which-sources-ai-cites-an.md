# Treg AEO toolkit shows which sources AI cites and who wrote them

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/treg_ai/status/2112137248200380614)** · `Treg` · 2026-10-19 · 80 replies · 103 reposts · 513 likes · 906 bookmarks · 94.5K views · 🎬 · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Treg launched an open-source AEO toolkit that finds every source an AI cites and the author behind it, then helps get your product into those sources - priced at 1 cent per prompt with no subscription and access to 4,000+ data/services. The repo is superdesigndev/treg, with CloroAI as partner. Replies treat the pricing as the story: 1 cent to 'win a prompt' in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini versus $50/month minimums elsewhere, and several users say Treg is already part of their SEO workflow. One describes it as an 'AEO agent' rather than another dashboard.

## 💡 Takeaway

A cheap, open-source way to inspect and influence the citations AI models actually use - more actionable than most AEO dashboards.
