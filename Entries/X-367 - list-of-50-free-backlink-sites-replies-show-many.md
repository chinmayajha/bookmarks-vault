# List of 50 'free' backlink sites; replies show many are paid or nofollow

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/wespreadjam/status/2115501442970857949)** · `jam` · 2026-10-28 · 33 replies · 42 reposts · 580 likes · 1.5K bookmarks · 57.7K views

## 💬 Essence

jam posts a list of 50 sites for backlinks to rank on Google and AI search, including Crunchbase, StackShare, G2, F6S, SourceForge, AlternativeTo, Product Hunt, Capterra, Wellfound, Dev.to, Hashnode, GitHub, Hugging Face, Smithery, mcp.so and many AI-tool directories. A self-reply promotes the author's own service. Replies give the reality check: one user tested the list on a product, found 12 public links from 50 platforms and only 2 without nofollow. Others say some sites are not free and do not approve submissions easily. Replies add more sites such as Indie Hackers, BetaList, DevHunt and OpenAlternative.

## 💡 Takeaway

A starting list of directories. Expect low yield: many are paid, slow, or nofollow. See the SaaS directory playbook entry.
