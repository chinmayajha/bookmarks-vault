# cubench: Cua Driver and agent-desktop lead a 5-tool computer-use benchmark

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/athrix_codes/status/2117430371650506835)** · `Athrix ☄️` · 2026-11-03 · 24 replies · 20 reposts · 339 likes · 346 bookmarks · 23.2K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Athrix benchmarks five computer-use tools on 40 real desktop tasks (forms, icons, tiny buttons, a calculator, a text editor) on one Ubuntu desktop, 8-core CPU and no GPU, checked against real app state. Tasks completed: Cua Driver 90% (36 of 40), agent-desktop 88% (35), UI-TARS 82% (33), OmniParser 52% (21), Open Interpreter 32% (13). Median time per action: agent-desktop 471 ms, Cua 1.1 s, Open Interpreter 2.8 s, OmniParser 53.6 s, UI-TARS 88.8 s. Said done but nothing happened: Cua 0 of 42, agent-desktop 1, OmniParser 1, UI-TARS 7, Open Interpreter 29. Peak memory: agent-desktop 90 MB, Cua 142 MB, Open Interpreter 1.41 GB, OmniParser 11.25 GB, UI-TARS 17.69 GB. The thread notes UI-TARS got 7 x 8 = 2 on the calculator. Replies praise agent-desktop, and its author says a new release will beat this.

## 💡 Takeaway

Accessibility-tree tools beat vision models on speed, memory and honest 'done' reports in this CPU-only test. See the two agent-desktop entries.
