# 24 years later, something better than JSON?

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/ChShersh/status/1882158898033160619)** · `Dmitrii Kovanikov` · 2025-01-22 · 48 replies · 362 likes · 61.1K views

## 💬 Essence

OP quips that it took 24 years to come up with something better than JSON, reacting to a new data format (CCL); the self-thread reframes CCL as a meta-language for building your own language on top, says cross-application compatibility must be ensured by tooling, compares it to YAML's ambiguity (one app may ignore case), and jokes about liking and quote-tweeting his own pinned posts. Replies are split: one reader who shared it with his team likes the idea but says undefined data types just push the parsing problem down the line (how do you parse a date?), others ask for the link, mock the self-promotion, or say 'fk this back to XML'.

## 💡 Takeaway

JSON-killer discourse with the sharpest critique (no type definitions) stated plainly.
