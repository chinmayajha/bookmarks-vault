## 💬 Essence

Serg pitches blogr.ai as an AI writer that does the whole loop rather than dumping random posts: it maps your niche, writes the articles, publishes them and watches the rankings, with the first article free. The post got almost no distribution - one bookmark and 879 views - which makes it a useful counterexample to the idea that launch copy alone creates traction. The same thread also promotes bytemail 1.0, a native Swift email app for Mac that handles Gmail, iCloud, Outlook, Yahoo and IMAP in one window with no web wrapper and no server in the mail path, suggesting Serg is shipping multiple small products rather than focusing on one.
