# fframes recreates a viral video with Rust, SVG and shaders - and beats the original's polish

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/neogoose_btw/status/2107645063895699515)** · `Dmitriy Kovalenko` · 2026-10-07 · 75 replies · 88 reposts · 2.2K likes · 2.1K bookmarks · 118.3K views · 🎬 · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Dmitriy Kovalenko answered a 'let's see AI recreate this' challenge by rebuilding the video as an fframes project with Codex, using only Rust, SVG and shaders. He argues the result is better than the original: fframes is faster, runs without a browser, avoids token waste on flex/grid layout, and has a built-in agentic verification loop for audio, collisions and motion. He pushed past the last 10% of detail and is open-sourcing the template. The thread's best tension is exactly that last 10% - the original creator's point is that taste is the hard part, and a reply pushes back that Kovalenko's transitions are actually better. People also want to know which model (Astra) and whether Codex needed docs for a niche framework.

## 💡 Takeaway

A concrete example of code-native video beating a general-purpose editor on iteration speed, with the taste question still unresolved.
