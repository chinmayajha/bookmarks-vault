# Treg open-sources people search for Claude at $0.0089 per lead

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/treg_ai/status/2109615287062745090)** · `Treg` · 2026-10-12 · 122 replies · 186 reposts · 2.5K likes · 6.5K bookmarks · 560.5K views · 🎬 · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Treg released an open-source people-search tool for Claude: $0.0089 per lead instead of $600 subscriptions, number one on People Search Bench, zero markup and fully open source. Setup is deliberately agent-native - paste 'set up treg - https://treg.to/llms.txt' into Claude or Codex - and the repo is the same superdesigndev/treg codebase behind its earlier AEO toolkit. The pitch lands because it unbundles one afternoon of research from a monthly subscription, which is exactly what the top replies say they wanted. Others report good results with Hermes and appreciate that Treg shows the cost of each action before running it, while B2B lead-list questions remain unanswered.

## 💡 Takeaway

A cheap, open alternative to subscription people-search tools; the per-lead pricing and pre-run cost display are the parts worth copying.
