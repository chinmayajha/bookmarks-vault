OP runs an experiment - one tip per like in 24 hours - and delivers a numbered masterclass on heuristic and bot contests: a disclaimer that 280 characters can't hold 15 years of experience, a free tip that everyone struggles with different subjects, that week-long contests leave time to do all your research during the contest (unlike CP, where you train for years), that you never need advanced math because you can always write simulation code (only Bayes' theorem excepted, 'I don't know calculus'), that beam search and simulated annealing are the only two techniques really worth knowing, that touch typing is priority #1 (10-30 hours to learn), the difference between H and B contests, and the best sites - Topcoder marathon and AtCoder AHC for heuristics, CodinGame for bots.
