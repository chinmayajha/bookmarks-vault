# An agent warms up a US TikTok account daily from a cron job on a cloud phone

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/yaa_priya/status/2112438664760606720)** · `Priya` · 2026-10-20 · 13 replies · 19 reposts · 223 likes · 339 bookmarks · 17.2K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

Priya says her agent warms up a US-based TikTok account every day, and she can turn the whole process into a cron job with one command to the agent. In the thread she says the workflow runs on Mobilerun, a cloud phone service (mobilerun.ai), that it can also run on a real device, and that her 4-month-old account still works because good infrastructure keeps TikTok from detecting it. Replies are doubtful: one says TikTok is very good at spotting bots and will shadow-ban the profile, and another asks whether it will trigger bot checks. Automated engagement can also break the platform's rules.

## 💡 Takeaway

An example of agents driving a phone on a schedule. The account-warming use is risky and may violate platform terms.
