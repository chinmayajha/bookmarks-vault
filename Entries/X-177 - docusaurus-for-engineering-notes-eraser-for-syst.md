## 💬 Essence

OP wanted a single home for his software engineering learnings - architecture, practices, system design, databases, networking, security, cloud and leadership - and settled on the open-source Docusaurus site; his follow-up adds that for system design he prefers eraser.io. Replies push back that Docusaurus suits SDK/framework docs but not image-heavy system-design or database documentation and can be slow at launch, one suggests multiplayer.app for interactive architecture diagrams and decisions, and others thank him or ask for the link.
