# SEO playbook: 5 clicks a month to 21,540 in 16 days, with caveats

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/noelcetaSEO/status/2123545906733584682)** · `Noel Ceta` · 2026-11-19 · 69 replies · 40 reposts · 697 likes · 1,828 bookmarks · 85.3K views

## 💬 Essence

Noel Ceta says a site went from 5 clicks a month to 21,540 in the first 16 days of November, while, he says, his feed is full of sites down 80% or more after Google's core update (started 10 Nov). His steps: (1) make the exact search phrase the H1 and title, turn H2s into questions and add an FAQ (10 to 17 questions). (2) Fix the basics first: one H1 per page (one page had 11), real links in menus, prerendered pages (response time from 0.5s+ to 0.1-0.2s). (3) Remove duplicate pages (22 product pages shared one title) and redirect the old URLs. (4) One page per comparison search, all built to one spec (136 pages, 8 sections, 6 FAQs, 8+ internal links). (5) Build hubs (16 hubs, 1,500-1,900 words each) and related-post blocks. (6) Publish data nobody else has, such as live averages from your own users. (7) Translate pages that already work, with hreflang and a review (15 languages, about 1,800 strings fixed). (8) Keep a human blog on a schedule. His rule: let AI type, not decide. He says no backlinks were built. Replies doubt the result: the climb took 16 months before the spike, and the site is not named, so nobody can check the chart.

## 💡 Takeaway

Sound on-page SEO basics, but the headline number is unverified. Use the steps, not the claim.
