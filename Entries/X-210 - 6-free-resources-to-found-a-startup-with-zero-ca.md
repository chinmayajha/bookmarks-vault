# 6 free resources to found a startup with zero capital in college

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/anshulforyou/status/1629441858911850496)** · `Anshul` · 2023-02-25 · 7 replies · 119 likes · 22.8K views

## 💬 Essence

OP says the common excuse that you need capital to start is wrong and shares the stack that let him found three companies in college with no capital: GitHub Student Pack (free access to paid hosting and analytics tools), Zoho Mail (five free domain mailboxes for a professional address), Freenom (a free domain for a year), AWS Free Tier plus a DigitalOcean cluster through the student pack, Notion for product management, and Miro's education plan for team collaboration. Replies say small startups mostly need skills and effort rather than capital, and add that college itself is a pool of co-founder resources.

## 💡 Takeaway

Zero-budget student founder toolkit - the free tiers are the part worth re-checking today.
