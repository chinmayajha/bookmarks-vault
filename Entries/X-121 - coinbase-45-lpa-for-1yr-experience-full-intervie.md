# Coinbase: 45+ LPA for 1yr experience, full interview breakdown

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/va_a14/status/1981819711529603278)** · `Vaibhav Agarwal` · 2025-10-24 · 17 replies · 52 reposts · 1.1K likes · 73.7K views · 714 bookmarks

## 💬 Essence

Vaibhav says Coinbase offers more than 45 LPA for engineers with 1+ years of experience, making it one of the highest-paying remote roles in India, and breaks down the SDE interview: a 90-minute CodeSignal OA with 4 medium DSA problems (3 of 4 usually advances), a short recruiter screen, then technical round 1 combining code mocking and DSA focused on clean modular extensible code, ... His thread debunks the myth that you work Diwali - he took a complete week off, as did teammates - and argues the salary goes far in India versus high-cost, high-tax Western locations. Replies call the salary life-changing but warn of personal sacrifices, counter that it's still peanuts relative to Western peers, and ask about finance-related roles; 1,065 likes and 714 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

Comp plus the full interview funnel for Coinbase India - and a real answer on work-life expectations for US-remote roles.
