# Four-prompt chain claims ChatGPT agent mode applied to 500 jobs overnight

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/nikola_mr64990/status/2117896466970906668)** · `Mr Nikola` · 2026-11-04 · 112 replies · 331 reposts · 3.6K likes · 6.6K bookmarks · 1.1M views

## 💬 Essence

Mr Nikola says ChatGPT applied to 500 jobs while he slept and 12 interview calls came within 24 hours. The thread gives the prompts. Step 1: upload your resume and ask ChatGPT to act as a senior recruiter and list 20 job titles you fit with the ATS keywords for each. Step 2: rewrite the resume into a master template with the Google XYZ formula and no red flags. Step 3: turn on Agent mode with the + sign so ChatGPT can open LinkedIn and Indeed. Step 4: ask it to find matching postings from the last 7 days and build a spreadsheet with links, match scores and a tailored resume version for each. The post has about 1.1M views, but the replies are mostly doubtful: 'clickbait', 'seems untrue', and jokes about cancelled interviews.

## 💡 Takeaway

Steps 1, 2 and 4 are a sound resume and tracking routine. The 500-applications and 12-calls result is unproven.
