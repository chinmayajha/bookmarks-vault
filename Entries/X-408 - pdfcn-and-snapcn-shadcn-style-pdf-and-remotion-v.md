# pdfcn and snapcn: shadcn-style PDF and Remotion video components from an unofficial account

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/shadcnlabs/status/2121850779862720617)** · `Shadcn Labs` · 2026-11-15 · 21 replies · 39 reposts · 1.3K likes · 2.3K bookmarks · 186.5K views

## 💬 Essence

The Shadcn Labs account posts two projects. pdfcn.dev is a set of PDF components built on Takumi and Forme that works with shadcn/ui and can render on the server. snapcn.dev is a shadcn registry of Remotion components for React video: text animations, captions, device mockups and full scenes, installed with the CLI under an MIT licence. The account says Claude can make launch videos with it. The pdfcn replies are harsh. Many call the output generic AI slop, and one reader says Word or PowerPoint templates look better for financial reports. Several say the name misleads, because the account is not affiliated with shadcn. The account answers that its bio says so. One reader asks for mobile preview.

## 💡 Takeaway

snapcn is the more useful of the two for video work. Neither project is an official shadcn project.
