# stageshow and limora: two design skills for coding agents

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/adrien_ninet/status/2124549512375279784)** · `Adrien Ninet` · 2026-11-22 · 6 replies · 7 reposts · 353 likes · 874 bookmarks · 24.4K views

## 💬 Essence

Adrien Ninet answers a quoted question from luna, who wants a design skill that makes a site look less like AI. He names two: stageshow (benji.org/stageshow) and the limora AI skills community (limora.ai), where you can also make your own. In his follow-up he says prompt quality sets design quality. You describe a UI element in your own words and get its real name, its API reference and a prompt for your coding agent. He calls this very useful when you recreate a design. Two readers agree: one backs stageshow, and one used a limora skill in a project.

## 💡 Takeaway

Two skills to try when agent-built UI looks generic. The proof is only reader praise.
