# Buy yourbrandreviews.com so LLMs quote reviews you control

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/ads4apps/status/2069971564078956858)** · `james` · 2026-06-25 · 17 replies · 15 reposts · 446 likes · 1.1K bookmarks · 59.8K views

## 💬 Essence

James suggests buying two domains - yourapp.com and yourappreviews.com - and hosting your own reviews there, so when users ask an LLM for reviews it pulls from a page you control. He calls it a dirty little secret everyone will do within 24 months. Replies are hostile and specific: no evidence is offered, the FTC's 2024 fake-review rule bans company-controlled sites posing as independent reviews (roughly $50k per violation), a new low-authority domain will not outrank Trustpilot/G2, and models can be updated to distrust it. He insists he never suggested fake reviews, only hosting real ones.

## 💡 Takeaway

A legally and ethically risky growth hack with no evidence behind it - worth knowing as a dark pattern, not worth copying.
