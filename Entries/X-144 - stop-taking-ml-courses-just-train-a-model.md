# Stop taking ML courses - just train a model

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/vikhyatk/status/1931196085508481370)** · `vik` · 2025-06-07 · 53 replies · 2.8K likes · 207.5K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

OP begs newcomers to ML to stop enrolling in yet another 'mathematics foundation III' online course and instead train something - finetune ResNet-50, do a Stable Diffusion LoRA, anything but another course. His self-thread concedes courses have value only when paired with real practice and that many confuse taking courses with learning, jokes a react-to-a-video course was more entertaining than Game of Thrones, and shares that his first attempt was a YouTube-to-MIDI piano model that kept pressing random keys. Replies agree they learned far more in practice than from the Coursera/Octave era, and one says just train bad, decent and good models.

## 💡 Takeaway

Popular build-first pushback against course-hoarding - useful counterweight to roadmap threads.
