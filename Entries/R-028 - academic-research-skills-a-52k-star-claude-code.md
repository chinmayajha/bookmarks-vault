## 💬 Essence

A BestGitHubRepos writeup of Imbad0202/academic-research-skills, a Claude Code plugin suite that covers research through publication with separate skills for each stage. Deep Research runs a 13-agent team with a Socratic guided mode, PRISMA systematic-review support, intent detection, dialogue-health monitoring, optional cross-model devil's advocate and Semantic Scholar verification. Academic Paper uses 12 agents for writing, style calibration, LaTeX hardening, visualization and citation conversion; Academic Paper Reviewer runs a 7-agent peer review with journal-fit, three dynamic reviewers and a devil's advocate; Academic Pipeline orchestrates 10 stages with checkpoints and claim verification. SR-Screener does protocol-driven screening with two blinded reviewers plus an adjudicator, kappa/PABAK QC and PRISMA 2020 counts. The repo reports 52,451 stars, 4,020 forks, v3.23.1, 32 contributors and 591 Claude-co-authored commits, with Edward Cheng-I Wu as top contributor. There are zero comments, so the numbers are the post's own claim rather than community-validated.
