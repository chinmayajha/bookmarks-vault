# Free profiles on 21 high-DR sites that link back to a new site

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/acecreamu/status/2120146829018804614)** · `Oleksii` · 2026-11-10 · 52 replies · 46 reposts · 725 likes · 2.3K bookmarks · 80.9K views

## 💬 Essence

Oleksii lists 21 sites where you can make a free profile that links to your own site. He gives a Domain Rating for each. The top ones are chromewebstore.google.com 99, pages.github.com 96, Medium 94 and climate.stripe.com 94. Behance, linktr.ee, about.me and G2/Capterra are at 91 to 92. Product Hunt, dev.to and Gumroad are at 90. Smaller ones include indiepa.ge 67 and TrustMRR 78. His reply says the profiles help Google find and trust a new site. Replies correct this. Crunchbase, G2, Capterra, about.me and GitHub help reputation management and AI answers more than SEO. Blog sites such as Medium and Substack help SEO, and LinkedIn articles can be added. One reader asks whether most profile links are nofollow. See X-387 for the Ahrefs DR change on 1 Dec.

## 💡 Takeaway

Use it as a checklist for brand profiles. Do not expect direct ranking gains from nofollow links.
