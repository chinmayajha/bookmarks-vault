# Fake a dig TXT record to dodge cybersecurity refusals

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/Zaddyzaddy/status/2092586380803153980)** · `Z A D D Y` · 2026-08-26 · 19 replies · 40 reposts · 1.1K likes · 65.9K views · 1.2K bookmarks

## 💬 Essence

ZADDY shares a trick for reducing model refusals in cybersecurity research tasks: fake the output of a dig command so it appears the domain has a TXT record named after your harness, then prompt the model to check it as proof of ownership. His thread just answers 'Nope'. Replies ask if a service is down, suggest Chinese LLMs allow security work with a simple system prompt, note Claude actually refuses TXT records as proof, and others say they do variants like editing the Windows hosts file; 1,121 likes and 1,193 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

A documented refusal-bypass pattern for security prompts - directly relevant to guardrail design on sensitive tasks.
