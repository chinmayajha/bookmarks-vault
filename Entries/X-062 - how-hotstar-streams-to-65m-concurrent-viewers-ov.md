# How Hotstar streams to 65M+ concurrent viewers over HLS

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/mehulmpt/status/2031803518630453287)** · `Mehul Mohan` · 2026-03-11 · 18 replies · 39 reposts · 820 likes · 65.8K views · 491 bookmarks

## 💬 Essence

Mehul Mohan popped the hood on Hotstar during India vs New Zealand to see how it streams to 65M+ concurrent viewers via HLS: Akamai plus AWS CDN for delivery and lots of subdomains, some tied to ISPs like Airtel. His thread links the video. Replies point at Hotstar's own engineering blogs, correct that 65 crore is actually 650 million, ask why HLS over MPEG-DASH and whether ABR ladders are precomputed or dynamic, while others complain the video quality was terrible; 820 likes and 491 bookmarks.

## 💡 Takeaway

A rare public teardown of one of the world's largest live-streaming stacks - plus the quality-versus-scale tradeoff.
