# Weespy Chrome extension reverse-spies a SaaS's traffic, ads and content

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/mathiaschpl/status/2111164098863862110)** · `Mathias Chapelon` · 2026-10-16 · 60 replies · 52 reposts · 313 likes · 459 bookmarks · 25.1K views · 🎬

## 💬 Essence

Mathias Chapelon launches Weespy, a free Chrome extension that shows how any SaaS gets customers: traffic estimates, tech stack, ads, SEO, and what competitors post on X, LinkedIn and Reddit, in one click. The thread walks through installing it, listing competitors, and copying formats that already perform. Replies ask whether traffic estimates come from SimilarWeb, whether it shows abandoned channels, and whether it can tailor social content to an audience - most go unanswered. The launch video itself was made with Opus 5.5 + Remotion.

## 💡 Takeaway

A free, fast competitor-research starting point - just treat traffic numbers as estimates and verify anything before betting a strategy on it.
