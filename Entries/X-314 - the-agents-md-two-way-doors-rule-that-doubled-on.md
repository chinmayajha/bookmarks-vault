# The AGENTS.md 'two-way doors' rule that doubled one dev's shipping

**[Open original ↗](https://x.com/daradoescode/status/2111150488980799536)** · `Dara A.` · 2026-10-16 · 37 replies · 39 reposts · 1.9K likes · 2.8K bookmarks · 68.2K views · ⭐ high follow-up

## 💬 Essence

Dara's AGENTS.md section tells agents: walk through two-way doors and show me after; stop at one-way doors. If the answer would be a rubber stamp, don't ask; if running something can answer the question, run it. Save questions for taste and product calls only, one at a time, with a recommendation, and 'no' is acceptable. Once a one-way door is opened it stays open for that work, including subagents, and the agent should return only if the door turns out different (more at stake, a check failing, something unknown). Replies focus on how much decision fatigue this removes and debate whether harnesses can be trusted with that autonomy.

## 💡 Takeaway

A compact, copy-pasteable autonomy contract that trades permission-seeking for stated reversibility - the best AGENTS.md snippet in this batch.
